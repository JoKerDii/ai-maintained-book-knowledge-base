# Radical Candor — Cheatsheet

## The quadrant: where is your guidance landing?
| | **Challenge Directly = NO** | **Challenge Directly = YES** |
|---|---|---|
| **Care Personally = YES** | **Ruinous Empathy** (most common mistake) | **RADICAL CANDOR** (goal) |
| **Care Personally = NO** | **Manipulative Insincerity** (worst) | **Obnoxious Aggression** (2nd best) |

**Decision rule**: If you're withholding criticism to avoid discomfort → you're in Ruinous Empathy; **just say it**. If you're being harsh without showing care → add care, don't stop challenging. Prefer Obnoxious Aggression to silence, but aim top-right.

## Tells you're in the wrong quadrant
- You say "good job" with no specifics → Ruinous Empathy praise.
- You're rehearsing how to soften it until you say nothing → Ruinous Empathy.
- You're thinking "you're stupid/lazy/greedy" → Obnoxious Aggression / personalizing.
- You reversed your position to be liked / avoid conflict → Manipulative Insincerity.
- Nobody is ever mad at you → you're not challenging enough.
- All praise, no criticism coming back at you → you're not soliciting well.

## Order of operations (rollout sequence)
1. **Share your stories** — explain Radical Candor in your own words; show vulnerability.
2. **Get criticism first** — prove you can take it before dishing it out (go-to question + silence).
3. **Career conversations** — start with longest-tenured reports (life story → dreams → 18-mo plan).
4. **Perfect 1:1s** in parallel (report owns agenda; you listen).
5. **Give impromptu guidance** — and gauge it.
6. **Assess** — don't add more until guidance + relationships + 1:1s feel solid.
7. **Staff / big-debate / big-decision meetings.**
8. **Encourage guidance between people**; prevent backstabbing.
9. **Fight meeting proliferation; think time.**
10. **Growth-management plans; reward rock stars.**
11. **Peer gauging; walk around; skip-levels (if manager of managers).**
12. **Radically Candid hiring, firing, promoting, reviews.**

## Giving guidance — HHIPP
**H**umble · **H**elpful · **I**mmediate · **I**n person · **P**raise in public / criticize in private · don't **P**ersonalize.
- Say it in **2–3 minutes** between meetings; don't save it for a 1:1/review.
- Use **SBI**: Situation → Behavior → Impact.
- Say **"that's wrong,"** not "you're wrong." Never Reply-All criticism.
- Worry *more* about praise (make it specific/sincere); for criticism, "just say it."

## Rock star vs. superstar
| | **Rock Star** (gradual growth) | **Superstar** (steep growth) |
|---|---|---|
| Wants | mastery, stability | new challenge, learning |
| Reward with | recognition, top ratings, guru/teaching roles, tenure honors | new opportunities, stretch projects, mentors |
| **Don't** | promote out of a loved role | block or squash ambition |
Both essential. No permanent labels — trajectories change with life.

## When to fire (three questions)
1. Have I given Radically Candid guidance, repeatedly, over time?
2. Do I understand the impact on the rest of the team?
3. Have I sought a second opinion?
→ Yes + no improvement = it's time. **The 4 lies that delay it**: "it'll get better," "somebody > nobody," "a transfer is the answer," "it's bad for morale." Firing rules: don't wait too long · don't decide alone · give a damn · follow up.

## GSD wheel (drive results)
**Listen → Clarify → Debate → Decide → Persuade → Execute → Learn.** Don't skip a step; don't get stuck on one. You're usually *not* the decider — push it to whoever's closest to the facts; get **facts, not recommendations**.

## Persuade = Aristotle
**Pathos** (the *listener's* emotions — most neglected) · **Ethos** (expertise + humility, "we" not "I") · **Logos** (show your work).

## Meeting rhythm
| Meeting | Purpose | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1:1** | listen/clarify | report sets agenda; **never cancel** |
| **Staff** | learn (metrics) / listen (snippets) / clarify (name debates) | no decisions here |
| **Big Debate** | wrestle | *not* deciding; decide-by date |
| **Big Decision** | decide | decider named; decision is final |
| **All-Hands** | persuade + hear dissent | Q&A > presentation |
| **Think Time** | think | sacred; no one schedules over it |

## Emotion first-aid (in a 1:1)
Acknowledge ("I can see you're upset") · ask questions · don't add your guilt · tissues a walk away · closed water bottles at hand · **walk, don't sit** · forgive yourself and step out if needed. Never say "don't take it personally / don't be sad."

## Core self-check (when lost)
> "Am I showing I **care personally**?" and "Am I **challenging** each person **directly**?" If both yes, you're doing fine.
