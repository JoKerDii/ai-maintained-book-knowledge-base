## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The Five Parts of Every Business** — the book's spine. A business is a repeatable process that (1) **creates value** people (2) **want or need** (Marketing), (3) at a price they'll **pay** (Sales), (4) **delivered to their satisfaction** (Value Delivery), (5) at **enough profit to keep going** (Finance). Diagnose any struggling venture by finding which of the five is broken — in order. If you can't describe all five, you don't have a business yet.

**The Iron Law of the Market** — market size and quality cap every business. A brilliant offer to a market that doesn't want it still fails. *Validate the market before perfecting the offer.*

**Value Creation — test before you build.** List your **Critically Important Assumptions (CIAs)**, build a **Minimum Economically Viable Offer (MEVO)**, and **Shadow Test** it (sell transparently before it exists). A preorder is the only feedback that counts; friends and family are noise. Value usually takes one of the **Twelve Standard Forms of Value** (Product, Service, Shared Resource, Subscription, Resale, Lease, Agency, Audience Aggregation, Loan, Option, Insurance, Capital). Improve via the **Iteration Cycle (WIGWAM)** — maximize iteration *velocity*, not bet size.

**Marketing — earn scarce Attention.** Make the offer **Remarkable** (worth remarking on). Sell the **End Result** ("holes, not drills"), not features. Reach the **Probable Purchaser** at their **Point of Market Entry**. Use a **Hook** (one-line benefit), a single **Call-to-Action**, and **Permission** (follow up after giving **Free** value). You can't manufacture **Desire**, only connect to it.

**Sales — support the price, build the Trust.** All prices are arbitrary but must be *supported* (**Pricing Uncertainty Principle**). Compute the **Four Pricing Methods** (Replacement Cost, Market Comparison, Discounted Cash Flow/NPV, **Value Comparison**) and **price on Value Comparison** — almost always the highest. Dissolve the five **Barriers to Purchase**; use **Education-Based Selling**, a **Damaging Admission**, and **Risk Reversal** (move risk from buyer to seller). In negotiation, power lies with whoever can walk away (**Next Best Alternative**).

**Value Delivery — Quality = Performance − Expectations.** Manage both sides. Customers want **Predictability**. Build a clean **Value Stream**, raise **Throughput**, and use **Systemization / Duplication / Multiplication / Scale** so improvements compound (**Accumulation**, **Amplification**). Every customer served is a **Barrier to Competition**.

**Finance — keep score, stay solvent.** Only **Four Methods to Increase Revenue** exist: more customers, bigger transactions, more frequency, higher prices. Spend up to your **Allowable Acquisition Cost (AAC)** (the marketing slice of **Lifetime Value**). Watch **Profit Margin**, **Overhead**, **Breakeven**, and above all **Purchasing Power** — run out of cash and it's game over. Ignore **Sunk Costs**; chase **Sufficiency** before scale.

**The Human Mind — work with the hardware.** **Perceptual Control**: people act like thermostats, moving only when a perception drifts past a **Reference Level**. Change behavior by changing the **Environment / Guiding Structure**, not by force. **Willpower** depletes (glucose-limited); **Loss Aversion** makes losses feel ~2× gains; **Absence Blindness** hides prevention and "doing nothing"; **Contrast** and **Scarcity** drive perceived value.

**Working With Yourself — manage attention, not time.** Seek **Monoidealism** (flow on one thing); kill the **Cognitive Switching Penalty**. Pick **2–3 MITs** daily. Use **Five-Fold Why** (find the real want) and **Five-Fold How** (reach a concrete **Next Action**). **Decide at 40–70%** of information. Reinvest ~5% of income in **Personal R&D**.

**Working With Others — influence and coordinate.** **Power** = influence or compulsion. Lead with **Commander's Intent** and a **Reason Why** so people adapt to **Change**. Treat everyone with the **Golden Trifecta** (appreciation, courtesy, respect). Keep teams **3–8** to limit **Communication Overhead**. When things go wrong, hold an **Option Orientation**.

**Systems — understand, analyze, improve.** Every working complex system evolved from a simple one (**Gall's Law**). Performance is capped by one **Constraint** — fix only that. Measure the **Critical Few KPIs** with **Context**; never confuse **Correlation and Causation**. Improve via the **Critical Few (80/20)**, reduce **Friction**, codify **SOPs/Checklists**, build **Fail-safes**, and stop at the **85% Solution**. Beware the **Paradox of Automation** (efficient automation needs *better* humans) and protect **Resilience** over raw Throughput.
