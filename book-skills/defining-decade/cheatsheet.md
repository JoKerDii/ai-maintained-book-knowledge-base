# Cheatsheet — The Defining Decade

Meg Jay's decision rules, thresholds, and tells — the judgment behind the frameworks.

## Core Decision Rules (if → then)
- **Choosing between options** → take the one with the most **identity capital** (skills + connections + a door-opening story), even if it's less fun or lower-paid now.
- **"I don't know what I want"** → it's a defense; assemble your real **six-flavor table** and choose from *those*. There is no 24-flavor table.
- **Speech full of "should / supposed to / less-than"** → you're in a **search for glory**; separate goals (inside, energizing) from shoulds (outside, oppressive) and drop the shoulds.
- **Seeking a job/intro/opportunity** → work your **weak ties** with a *specific, researched* favor, not your close friends and not a vague "coffee???"
- **Rejecting a path because it's "ordinary"** → that's the **should-not**; ask "What am I *for*?" and claim it. Different is simple; affirmative is brave.
- **Applying/interviewing** → lead with a coherent **story** (past→present→next) that *makes sense*, not a list.
- **Considering moving in together** → **decide, don't slide**; have the commitment conversation first. Ideally don't cohabit before engagement.
- **A work/relationship jolt makes you catastrophize** → **reappraise**: "What about the facts?" A feeling isn't a command. Jobs/relationships usually aren't that fragile.
- **Low confidence at a new job** → you can't feel it before you earn it; **bank mastery experiences**, count toward 10,000 hours, seek feedback.
- **Feeling stuck / "at loose ends"** → **life transplant**: make concrete adult commitments; don't wait to feel like an adult before acting.
- **Future feels far off / goals tossed decades away** → **do the math** with a timeline, working backward from what you want.

## What to Be Picky About in a Partner (Being in Like)
| Be picky about (20-year things) | Let go of (everyday things) |
|---|---|
| Extreme differences in **values / goals** | Unread book on the nightstand |
| **Personality** mismatch (esp. high Neuroticism) | "Not funny enough at parties" |
| Whether you **love** each other | Forgot the lilies / corny in-law gifts |
| Whether you genuinely **like** who they are | Different taste in weekend activities |

**Rule**: Obvious similarities (age, religion, politics) are *deal breakers, not match makers*. Personality is the real match maker — and where couples are least alike.
**Red flag**: High **Neuroticism** predicts breakup more than any dissimilarity. How you handle differences > the differences.

## Tells & Smells (recognize the situation fast)
- **Shame story running the show** → *secrecy*: they won't mention a partner/job until it blows up; never even thought to tell a best friend.
- **Dating/working down** → choices that contradict who the person actually is now.
- **A self-cure gone bad** → a coping habit (music, hookups, numbing) that once helped now isolates. "Every problem was once a solution — put the raft down."
- **Sliding, not deciding** → "it just happened"; no conversation, ring, or decision behind a big life change.
- **Present bias** → love/work described as "way far off," lots of distraction, hanging with people who don't talk about the future.
- **Availability heuristic** → reasoning from a couple of anecdotes / Hollywood headlines instead of base rates.
- **Leaf in the wind** → mood tracks the last thing a boss said; work taken intensely personally.

## The Big Five (low ↔ high)

| **C**onscientiousness | easygoing, careless, spontaneous | disciplined, organized, dutiful |

## Numbers & Thresholds to Remember
- **Frontal lobe** matures between **~20 and 30**.
- **~10,000 hours** to expertise ≈ 5 yrs focused full-time (or 10 yrs part-time).
- **Marriage age**: past ~**25**, age at marriage no longer predicts divorce. Divorce rate ~**40%**.
- **Fertility vs. your twentysomething self**: ~½ at **30**, ¼ at **35**, ⅛ at **40**. Per-cycle odds ~20–25% to 35, ~5% at 40.
- **Miscarriage**: ~¼ after 35, ~½ after 40.
- **Cohabitation effect** is *pre-engagement* only — living together *after* a public commitment carries no penalty.
- **Wage growth**: ~⅔ of lifetime wage growth happens in the first **10 years** of a career.
- **Weak ties**: >¾ of new jobs come from contacts seen "occasionally" or "rarely."

## The One-Line Ethos
"Claim your adulthood. Be intentional. Get to work. Pick your family. Do the math. Make your own certainty. **You are deciding your life right now.**"
