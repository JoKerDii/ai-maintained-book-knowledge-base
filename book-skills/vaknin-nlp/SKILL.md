## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**NLP is a methodology, not its techniques.** The power is the modeling method; the 350+ patterns are its products. Mastery = running a pattern from its underlying logic (the gestalt), not memorized steps — "practice scales to gain improvisational freedom."

**The 21 Presuppositions** are NLP's operating beliefs. The decisive ones for change-work:
- *The map is not the territory* — work with people's internal models, always improvable.
- *#12 The meaning of communication is the response you get* — calibrate to the response, not your intention.
- *#14 There is no failure, only feedback* — convert every miss into a correction.
- *#15 The most flexible element controls the system* — expand your behavioral choices.
- *#16 Resistance signals lack of rapport* — back up and reconnect, don't push.
- *#8 Every behavior has utility in some context* — find the positive intention inside the problem.
- *#17 People have the resources they need* — you direct the process; their brain does the change.

**The change-work loop**: 1) choose the right pattern → 2) work the client through it → 3) accept and use whatever happens (utilization) → 4) compare feedback to the outcome → 5) proceed (re-pattern or finish).

**The master pattern-selection logic** (full table in [cheatsheet.md](cheatsheet.md)):
- Habit on a trigger → **The Swish** (Ch 5): big/bright associated trigger ↔ small/dim dissociated self-image, swap fast, ×5–7, test.
- Irrational fear/trauma → **Phobia Cure / V-K dissociation** (Ch 8): B&W movie from the projection booth, then rewind fast in color, associated.
- Inner conflict → **Six Step Reframe / Parts work** (Ch 6): contact the part, honor its positive intention, generate better behaviors, ecology-check.
- Limiting belief → **Belief Chaining / Re-Imprinting / Meta-Model** (Ch 7, Ch 9).
- Need a state on demand → **Anchoring / Circle of Excellence** (Ch 4): anchor at the PEAK with a unique trigger.
- Want rapport → **Pace then lead** (Ch 3): match world first, then move them.
- Influence in conversation → **Milton-Model** (Ch 10): artful vagueness, embedded commands, double binds, pace–link–lead.
- Gather precise info / dissolve vagueness → **Meta-Model** (Ch 9): the inverse — recover deletions, distortions, generalizations.

**Two inverse language models**: the **Meta-Model** chunks *down* to specifics (recovers deep structure → solutions); the **Milton-Model** chunks *up* to artful vagueness (induces trance → the listener fills gaps with their own meaning). Same map, opposite directions.

**Always ecology-check before locking in a change.** Unaddressed secondary gain or systemic impact silently sabotages results — find the positive intention, verify no part objects.

**Edit the driver submodality, not the content.** A feeling's intensity lives in size/brightness/location/light, not in the story. Find that knob first.

**Change at the highest relevant logical level** (Dilts: Environment → Behavior → Capability → Beliefs/Values → Identity → Spirit). A problem felt at Identity won't yield to a Behavior fix.

> ⚠️ **Scope/safety**: NLP is not a substitute for medical, psychiatric, or psychological treatment. Refer clinical phobia, trauma, addiction, and severe grief to licensed professionals.
