## Core decision rules

- **Before any change, run an ecology check.** Unaddressed secondary gain or system impact silently sabotages the result. (Ch 1, Ch 2)
- **Resistance = lack of rapport.** If you meet resistance, stop pushing and rebuild rapport. (Presupposition #16, Ch 3)
- **The meaning of your communication is the response you get** — calibrate to the response, not your intention. (Presupposition #12)
- **Change at the highest relevant logical level.** A problem felt at Identity won't yield to a Behavior fix; a behavior issue doesn't need an identity overhaul. (Ch 12)
- **Find the positive intention before removing any behavior or belief** — removal without it relapses or leaves a void. (Ch 6, Ch 7)
- **Edit the driver submodality, not the content.** The charge lives in size/brightness/location/light — find that knob first. (Ch 5, Ch 8)
- **Pace before you lead** — match the person's world (3 verifiable facts / their physiology / their values), then move them. (Ch 3, Ch 10)
- **Chunk UP for agreement, DOWN for solutions.** Vague is undisputed; specific is actionable. (Ch 9)
- **Phrase suggestions positively** — the mind can't process a negation ("non-smoker" cues smoking). (Ch 10, Ch 12)
- **Anchor at the PEAK with a unique, dedicated trigger** — peak + purity + uniqueness = the strongest anchor. (Ch 4)
- **Accept and use whatever happens** (utilization) — interruptions, resistance, and odd responses are raw material, not derailment. (Ch 1, Ch 10)

## The 21 Presuppositions — the decisive ones for change-work

- #8 Every behavior has utility in *some* context → look for the gift inside the problem.
- #12 The meaning of communication is the response you get → own the outcome.
- #14 There is no failure, only feedback → mine every miss.
- #15 The most flexible element controls the system → expand your choices.
- #16 Resistance signals lack of rapport → back up and reconnect.
- #17 People have the internal resources they need → your job is to direct, not supply.

## Meta-Model quick reference (recover the deleted/distorted)

| Hear this | Violation | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| "never / always / everyone" | Universal Quantifier | "Never? Not once?" |
| "I can't / I must" | Modal Operator | "What stops you?" / "What if you did?" |
| "they / people / it" (vague) | Lack of Referential Index | "Who, specifically?" |
| "the relationship / my fear" | Nominalization | Turn it back into a verb — "relating how?" |
| "she makes me angry" | Cause–Effect | "How does what she does cause your anger?" |
| "I know he doesn't care" | Mind Reading | "How do you know?" |
| "It's wrong to…" | Lost Performative | "According to whom?" |

## Safety & ethics tells

- **Refer clinical cases** (phobia, trauma, addiction, severe grief) to licensed professionals — NLP is not a treatment substitute. (Ch 8, Ch 13)
- **"Poor participant" is often a brain shutdown, not stubbornness** — Think Physiology and consider referral. (Ch 13)
- **All communication should increase choice** (Presupposition #19) — use Milton patterns to expand options, never to coerce. (Ch 10)
- **Stay the formal authority, one outcome at a time** — don't befriend early or work 10 issues at once. (Ch 1)
