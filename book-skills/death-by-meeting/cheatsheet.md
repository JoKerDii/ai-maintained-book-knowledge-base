# Death by Meeting — Cheatsheet

## Diagnose first: which problem is this?
| Symptom | Root problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting is **boring**, disengaged, polite | Lack of **drama** | Mine for conflict; add the hook |
| Meeting is **ineffective**, nothing decided | Lack of **contextual structure** | Split into the four meetings |
| People want different things from one meeting | Meeting **stew** | Separate by purpose/format/timing |
| Lots of post-meeting email/hallway clarifying | **Sneaker time** | Get clarity/closure/buy-in in the room |

## The Four Meetings — pick by purpose
| If you need to… | Use | Time | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coordinate today's activities | **Daily Check-in** | 5 min, standing | Daily |
| Resolve this week's tactics | **Weekly Tactical** | 45–90 min | Weekly |
| Decide a big long-term issue | **Monthly Strategic** | ~2 hrs/topic | Monthly |
| Decide a big issue that *can't wait* | **Ad Hoc Strategic** | as needed | On demand |
| Step back & review the whole business | **Quarterly Off-Site** | 1–2 days | Quarterly |

## Weekly Tactical — run it in this order
1. **Lightning Round** — each person, 2–3 priorities, **≤60 sec** (~10 min)
2. **Progress Review** — 4–6 key metrics, clarify only (~5 min)
3. **Real-Time Agenda** — set *now*, from what's actually happening (~15 min in)
> Rule: strategic topic raised? **Park it** for the Monthly Strategic. Don't discuss.

## Decision rules
- **When a meeting feels flat → mine for conflict.** Surface the disagreement everyone
  is avoiding; politeness is the disease, not the cure.
- **When first conflict gets tense → grant real-time permission.** Interrupt: "this is
  exactly the debate we need — keep going."
- **When a strategic issue erupts in a tactical meeting → park it,** don't solve it.
- **When a strategic issue truly can't wait → call an Ad Hoc Strategic,** separate from
  the Weekly Tactical.
- **When tempted to pre-write the Weekly agenda → don't.** Use disciplined spontaneity.
- **When someone says "too many meetings" → count the sneaker time** the bad meetings
  create. Better meetings save net time.
- **When the Daily Check-in runs long → stay standing, end hard,** park the rest.

## Thresholds & defaults
- Daily Check-in: **5 min** (hard-cap ~10), **standing**, never cancel.
- Lightning round: **60 sec/person**; 2–3 priorities.
- Progress review: **~5 min**, **4–6 metrics** max.
- Monthly Strategic: **≥2 hrs/topic**, **1–2 topics** only, prep required.
- Off-site: **1–2 days**, ~**1 hour's drive** away (not Hawaii), **no outsiders**
  (except a trusted facilitator).
- New meeting type: commit **~2 months** before judging it.

## Tells & smells (you're in trouble if…)
- The leader optimizes for **ending on time** and **avoiding tension**.
- The **agenda was emailed 48 hrs early** and no one replied.
- One **2–3 hour "staff meeting"** covers tactics + strategy + admin + culture.
- People lobby to end the meeting to "go do **real work**."
- Disagreements happen **in the hallway**, never in the room.
- The off-site is mostly **slides** or mostly **golf**.

## Off-Site: four reviews to cover
Strategy · Team · Personnel (stars & poor performers) · Competition/Industry
