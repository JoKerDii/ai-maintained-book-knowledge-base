## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The Big Cycle** — Empires rise and decline in a recognizable, cause-driven arc (~250 yrs), an "upward corkscrew" of an evolutionary productivity uptrend with cycles around it. Locate any country on it (Rise / Top / Decline) by rating its determinants; the *average* reading defines the stage. Most events are "another one of those" — if you lack history's patterns in mind, you'll miss the current shift.

**The three big cycles (watch these first)** — (1) money/credit/debt, (2) internal order/disorder, (3) external order/disorder. All three good → strong & rising; all three bad → weak & declining. Add innovation and acts of nature for the "Big Five."

**The 18 determinants / 8 measures of power** — Education → innovation/tech → competitiveness → output & trade share → military → financial center → **reserve currency** (rises last, falls last). These strengths are mutually reinforcing — they rise and fall together. Human capital is the most sustainable capital ("self-sufficient plus" = produce more than you spend).

**The long-term debt cycle (~50–75 yrs, 6 stages)** — hard money → paper claims → rising debt → crisis/devaluation → fiat → flight back to hard money. Monetary systems rotate Type 1 (hard) → 2 (paper claims) → 3 (fiat) → back. **"Debt eats equity — but central banks feed debt by printing."** The 4 levers to cut debt burdens: austerity → defaults → transfers/taxes → **print & devalue** (most common). Aim for a "beautiful deleveraging."

**Currency risk = the biggest blind spot.** "All currencies devalue or die" (only ~20% of ~750 since 1700 survive, all devalued). Reserve status is the exorbitant privilege that sows its own demise via over-borrowing; it declines "slowly then suddenly." Late-cycle, the danger isn't debtor default — it's creditors holding assets **devalued below inflation.**

**The 6 internal-order stages (~100 yrs)** — new order → build systems → peace/prosperity → excess → bad finances/conflict → civil war → repeat. Diagnose like disease stages. **Lead indicator of civil war = bankrupt government + big wealth gaps.** Classic Toxic Mix = bad finances + large wealth/values gaps + a negative shock. Watch Stage-5 tells: decadence, bureaucracy, populism/polarization, scapegoating, loss of truth in media, rules → raw fighting (blood spilled = Stage 6).

**The external-order cycle** — International relations run on raw power ("law of the jungle"), not law. 5 kinds of war: trade → technology → capital → geopolitical → military (the first four precede shooting by ~5–10 yrs). Highest war risk = comparable power + irreconcilable existential differences. **Have power, respect power, use power wisely** (soft power often beats hard); fight early if declining, later if rising. Win-win (grow and divide the pie) beats lose-lose.

**Investing** — All markets driven by growth, inflation, risk premiums, discount rates. Risk = failing to meet your needs, not volatility. Beware survivorship bias (don't extrapolate US/UK only). Bet on evolution's upside, but not so much a bump knocks you out. When money is printed cheaply, own assets; in war, sell debt and buy gold.

**Current verdict (2021)** — US ~70% through its Big Cycle, internal Stage 5, ~30% civil-war-dynamic odds in 10 yrs; China Stage 3 and rising; US-China in 4 of 5 wars with ~35% odds of a big war (held down by mutually assured destruction). "Peace is profitable, war is costly." The biggest war is the **war with ourselves.**
