# Glossary — The Essential Psycho-Cybernetics

**Active Forgiveness** — Forgiveness as an all-out daily operation rather than a word; includes forgiving wrongs you only *imagine* were done to you (Ch 7).

**Affirmation** — A written present-tense statement used to feed the subconscious; governed by nine rules (personal, positive, present, non-progressive, non-comparative, no "can," balanced, realistic, no time limits) (Ch 14).

**As if** — Acting in imagination the way you intend to be. "Act the way you want to be (in your imagination), and you'll be the way you act" (Ch 14, Ch 17).

**Automatic Success Mechanism** — The goal-striving servo-mechanism when fed success goals; impersonal, automatic, supplies the *how* once you supply the objective (Ch 17).

**Comfort zone / effectiveness range** — The performance band around your self-image. Performance outside it produces tension and reverts (Ch 12).

**Compassion** — The pivot quality; the second C of S-U-C-C-E-S-S. "With compassion, you are somebody; without compassion, you are nobody" (Ch 1, Ch 24).

**Creative Mirror Watching** — Looking past your face to the person you intend to become; distinguished from narcissism and self-prosecution (Ch 8).

**Daily Dozen (12 Ways to a New Self-Image)** — Book Four's twelve instruments: Truth, Imagination, Relaxation, That Winning Feeling, Good Habits, The Aim of Happiness, Unmasking, Compassion, Accepting Your Weakness, Living with Your Mistakes, Being Yourself, Never Retiring (Ch 23).

**Daily Dozen for Tranquility** — Twelve forces to fight, spelling TRANQUILITY: Tension, Resistance, Arrogant aggressiveness, Negation, Quandary, Unbelief, Inferiority, Life of loneliness, Laissez-faire, Irritation, Trepidation, Yesterday Incorporated (Ch 9).

**Daily vacation** — A flight into the quiet room of your mind taken *every day*, not annually (Ch 22).

**Decompression chamber** — A designated room in the mind for regrouping under frustration (Ch 3).

**Delay Your Response** — Count slowly to 12 at the moment you know anger can no longer be checked; aim is control of action, not absence of feeling (Ch 22).

**Descriptive Statement** — The detailed written "why" of a goal, written before the short affirmation (Ch 14).

**Divine Discontent** — Negative feedback signalling you are off your ultimate heading (Ch 11).

**Emotional scars** — Invisible injuries, deeper and harder to remove than physical scars, which generate new injury until you resolve to heal them (Ch 17, Ch 19).

**Failure Mechanism / F-A-I-L-U-R-E** — Fear, Anger, Inferiority, Loneliness, Uncertainty, Resentment, Emptiness. The same machine as the success mechanism, fed negative aims (Ch 3, Ch 24).

**Five Rules for Winning Friends** — Be a friend to yourself; reach out; imagine you're the other person; accept their individuality; meet their needs (Ch 21).

**Five Roadblocks of Frustration** — Worrying after the decision; worrying about yesterday and tomorrow; too many things at once; wrestling problems around the clock; refusing to relax (Ch 24).

**Four Principles of Relaxation** — Forgive others; forgive yourself; see yourself at your best; keep up with yourself (Ch 4).

**Goal Builders** — Fourteen weekly exercises (six days on, one off) converting Book Two's chapters into physical practice (Ch 5–10).

**Happiness Habit** — Happiness as a cultivable thinking habit and a by-product of goals, never a target aimed at directly (Ch 3, Ch 16).

**Keep up with yourself** — Compare only against your own past, never another person's present (Ch 4).

**Kybernetes** — Greek for "helmsman"; the root of *cybernetics* and the source of the steering metaphor (Ch 11).

**Mañana Incorporated** — The membership of people who defer everything to a tomorrow nobody has experienced. Members never get dividends (Ch 2, Ch 6).

**The Method** — Ten minutes daily, eyes closed, body still, imagining yourself successful in vivid multi-sensory detail as a *participant*; 21 consecutive days (Ch 14).

**Mirror Magic Practice** — Six-day drills attached to existing daily events: morning breathing and the friend question, garbage disposal, closing the door on the past, the red traffic light (Ch 16).

**Miracle drugs (four)** — For emotional scars: accept your limitations; forget your mistakes; forgive others; see yourself at your best (Ch 19).

**Negative feedback** — A signal that you are off course. "Never a STOP sign"; its purpose is to adjust direction, not halt action (Ch 15).

**Peace of mind** — The port Psycho-Cybernetics steers toward — not achievement (Ch 11).

**Positive doing** — Maltz's correction to positive thinking; the method is a practice, not a reading (Ch 3).

**Psycho-Cybernetics** — Steering your mind to a productive, useful, worthwhile, predetermined goal (Ch 11).

**Quiet Place** — A retreat constructed in imagination, furnished to your taste, entirely private, entered at least once a day (Ch 13).

**Retirement Mechanism** — Ten components of withdrawal from life, each paired with an alternative: Retreat→Return, Eviction→Conviction, Withdrawal→Courage to Act, →Imaginative Power, →Reaffirmation, →Responsibility, Mañana→Today, Ennui→Emancipation, No direction→Goal Orientation, Traitor→Friend (Ch 9).

**Self-image** — The master framework: the picture you have of yourself; the ceiling on the effectiveness with which you can use your true potential. Behavior, feelings and abilities are always consistent with it (Ch 12).

**Servo-mechanism** — The subconscious understood not as a mind but as an automatic goal-striving machine directed by consciousness (Ch 17).

**Seven Rules for Happy Living** — Get the happiness habit; declare war on negative feelings; strengthen your self-image; learn how to laugh; dig out your buried treasures; help others; seek activities that make you happy (Ch 19).

**Success Cycle** — Success leading to success leading to more success; the constructive counterpart of the failure cycle (Ch 4).

**S-U-C-C-E-S-S** — Sense of direction, Understanding, Courage, Compassion, Esteem, Self-acceptance, Self-confidence (Ch 4, Ch 24).

**Synthetic experience** — Manufactured success experience, valid because the nervous system cannot distinguish a vividly imagined event from a real one. Requires Desire, Information, Repetition (Ch 14).

**Theater of the mind** — The staging device for all mental rehearsal: you are producer, director, writer, actor, technician and audience (Ch 17).

**Therapeutic forgiveness** — Cancelling the debt with no reservations, because you come to see the debt itself was never valid (Ch 24).

**Think Sheets** — Written self-interrogation exercises for surfacing goals and assumptions (Ch 14).

**Tomorrow Illusion** — Deferring your life to a day that never arrives; frustration as the thief of time (Ch 2).

**Twelve Faces of Frustration** — Book One's diagnostic list of frustration's forms (Ch 1); mirrored by the **Twelve Faces of Success** (Ch 4).

**Winning feeling** — The maintained sense of being someone who deserves success; feeds the automatic success mechanism (Ch 23).

**Wonderful computer** — Maltz's name for the goal-striving mechanism in the mid-brain (Ch 3).

**Yesterday Tomorrow Complex** — Living roughly 60% in yesterday and 20% in tomorrow, leaving almost nothing for today (Ch 9, Ch 24).

**21-day rule** — "For a person to experience a noticeable change in a mental picture, a minimum of twenty-one consecutive days must elapse" (Ch 11).
