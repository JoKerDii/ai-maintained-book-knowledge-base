# Cheatsheet — Which mechanism am I running, and what do I do now?

## The one diagnostic question
**Am I feeding this machine a goal, or feeding it my inferiority?** Same servo-mechanism either way. It is impersonal and it executes whatever picture it gets.

## Which mechanism am I running?

| Tell | Mechanism | Do this now |
|---|---|---|
| I'm rehearsing how it goes wrong | Failure (worry = rehearsal reversed) | Same technique, flip the content. The Method, 10 min. |
| I'm straining, pushing, gripping | Failure (trying too hard) | Stop. Ask "what am I actually accomplishing at this pace?" Accept the limit. |
| I keep replaying the injury | Failure (Resentment) | Ask what the condemnation is buying you. Then cancel the debt. |
| I'm waiting for tomorrow / better conditions | Failure (Mañana Inc.) | Do the constructive thing today. Postpone only the resentment. |
| I have no goal for today | Failure (Emptiness) | Write one goal, however small. That is the whole intervention. |
| I did well and now feel uneasy | Comfort-zone tension | Normal. You left the effectiveness range. Raise the picture, don't retreat. |
| I'm calm and aimed at something | Success | Continue; stoke the winning feeling. |

## F-A-I-L-U-R-E → what each needs
**F**ear → a goal (converts fear to growth) · **A**nger → count to 12 · **I**nferiority → self-image work, not skill training · **L**oneliness → compassion; reach out · **U**ncertainty → a goal bends it · **R**esentment → audit the anxiety underneath · **E**mptiness → direction

## S-U-C-C-E-S-S → the build order
**S**ense of direction · **U**nderstanding · **C**ourage · **C**ompassion · **E**steem · **S**elf-acceptance · **S**elf-confidence

## Decision rules

- **If effort isn't producing results → stop adding effort.** Change the self-image first. Behavior is always consistent with the picture; work outside the effectiveness range produces tension and reversion, not growth.
- **If affirmations feel like lying → they will not work.** "When it is incompatible with the self-image, it absolutely will not work." Do The Method before the recitation.
- **If you made a mistake → it's a course correction, never a stop sign.** Aware → evaluate the *outcome* as unwanted → correct **at once**. The "at once" is what makes it a reflex; a review period makes it rumination.
- **If the decision is made → stop the worrying.** Anxiety is creative *while* choosing among options and is dead weight after. Call on past successes instead.
- **If you can't solve it → sleep *on* it, not with it.**
- **If you're doing three things → you're doing none.** One thing, one thought, one goal at a time.
- **If you can't relax → check two roadblocks only.** (1) Are you refusing your limitations? (2) Are you holding a grudge? "Compassion and love are parents of relaxation." You cannot have peace of mind while you hate.
- **If you're nervous before a performance → don't fix the nerves.** Fix the expectation of failure. Accept the fear explicitly, then recall triumphs.
- **If you're inhibited around people → you're over-monitoring.** Stop trying to impress; stop calculating effect; stop thinking about what they think.
- **If a limit has stopped you for years → check whether the glass is still there.** The pike starves beside minnows; the elephant stays at a stake it could uproot.
- **If you can't forgive → find your payoff first.** "As long as we condemn another, we can feel superior to him." Name the benefit, then cancel with no reservations.
- **If you're punishing yourself for a mistake → ask what you gain.** There is no answer. Be kind to yourself instead.
- **If you're comparing yourself to someone → wrong comparison class.** Keep up with *yourself*.

## Thresholds & defaults

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum before judging any self-image change | **21 consecutive days** |
| Daily mental rehearsal | **10 minutes** |
| Goal Builder cadence | **1 per week — 6 days on, 1 off** |
| Micro-drill cadence (Mirror Magic) | **6 days** |
| Anger delay | **count to 12** |
| Quiet-place entry | **at least once daily** |
| Vacation frequency | **daily**, not annual |
| Goals per day | **at least one, however small** |
| Deadlines | on written **plans** — never on affirmations ("time limits are deadly; they create tension") |

## Tells & smells

- **"I'll start tomorrow"** → Mañana Inc. You are a member and you will get no dividends.
- **"I read the book"** → passive reading. Positive *doing* is the method.
- **"I don't feel better"** after the external problem is fixed → the scar was never the physical one.
- **Envy inside your admiration of someone** → your self-respect is low; Rule 1 first.
- **Hurt by what you *think* they think** → a feedback loop with no external input.
- **"I'm just being realistic about myself"** → check whether you are sampling only failures. Truth about yourself is usually false, and biased downward.
- **Calm on the surface, no rest inside** → concealment, not relaxation.
- **A grudge older than a few days** → past the cleansing window; now it's the disease.
- **Someone who dropped the script and didn't flinch** → their self-image held. That's the only variable under load.

## The four principles of relaxation (memorize)
1. Forgive others. 2. Forgive yourself. 3. See yourself at your best. 4. Keep up with yourself.
