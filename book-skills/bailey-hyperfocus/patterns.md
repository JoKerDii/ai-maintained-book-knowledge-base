# Hyperfocus — Patterns & Techniques

## Sort Tasks into the Four Quadrants
**When to use**: Whenever you feel busy but unproductive.
**How**: Map monthly tasks by productive/unproductive × attractive/unattractive → necessary, purposeful, distracting, unnecessary. Live in the top two.
**Trade-offs**: A "stepping-back" activity — you must remove yourself from the trenches to do it (5–10 min).

## The Four Stages of Hyperfocus
**When to use**: Any complex, purposeful task.
**How**: (1) Choose a meaningful object; (2) eliminate internal + external distractions in advance; (3) focus for a preset time; (4) draw attention back each time it wanders.
**Trade-offs**: For complex tasks only — full attention on a habit makes you choke.

## Rule of 3
**When to use**: Every morning.
**How**: Pick the three things you want done by day's end; keep them visible. Optionally set 3 weekly and 3 personal intentions too.

## Most Consequential Tasks
**When to use**: A long to-do list.
**How**: Rank by second/third-order consequences — which task is the domino that topples a hundred others? Combine with the quadrant grid.

## Implementation Intentions
**When to use**: Any difficult or important intention.
**How**: Specify when/where/which-action ("Go to the gym" → "go on my lunch break"). Name the situational cue.
**Trade-offs**: Overkill for trivial goals; essential for hard ones. You must actually care about the goal.

## Hourly Awareness Chime
**When to use**: To stay intentional moment-to-moment.
**How**: Set an hourly timer; when it rings, check whether you're on autopilot, how full your attentional space is, and whether you're on the most consequential thing. Pick 2–3 prompts.

## Distraction-Free Mode
**When to use**: Before any hyperfocus block.
**How**: Block distracting sites/apps (Freedom, Cold Turkey, RescueTime), phone in Do Not Disturb in another room, noise-canceling headphones, maybe caffeine. When a new distractor slips through, add it to the block list.
**Trade-offs**: Impulsive people may find blockers stressful — use for the hardest tasks and treat yourself after.

## Tame the Five Pain Points
**When to use**: Ongoing, to reduce baseline distraction.
**How**: Notifications (disable nonessential) · Phone (treat as "a more annoying computer"; stow it; second "distractions device"; phone swap) · Email (batch at set times, five-sentence rule, delete phone app, external to-do list, 20-min hyperfocus bursts) · Meetings (no agenda→no meeting; question recurring; challenge attendee list) · Internet (disconnect periodically).

## Clear Your Mind (Externalize Open Loops)
**When to use**: When nagging thoughts fragment your focus.
**How**: Offload to a to-do list, calendar, distractions list, waiting-for list, worry list (Getting Things Done). Removes guilt (past), worry (future), doubt (present).

## The Three Styles of Scatterfocus
**When to use**: To plan, recharge, or connect ideas.
**How**: **Capture** (roam free, write what surfaces — clear the mind); **Problem-crunching** (circle one big problem — use sparingly, 30–60 min); **Habitual** (simple enjoyable task + capture ideas — most powerful, most often). Keep a notepad.

## Refreshing Breaks (90/15 Rule)
**When to use**: Throughout the workday.
**How**: Break at least every 90 min, ~15 min per hour; make it low-effort, wanted, and not a chore ("pleasurably effortless"). Not email/news. Take breaks *before* you need them.

## Connect More Dots
**When to use**: Stuck on a creative or nonlinear problem.
**How**: Scatter in a richer environment; write the problem down; sleep on it; step back/unfocus; leave the task unfinished (stop mid-sentence); consume more valuable dots.

## Consumption Audit
**When to use**: Periodically, to upgrade your inputs.
**How**: Log everything you consume into useful/balanced/entertaining/trashy. Cut trash, add a valuable item for each cut, veg out with intention, invest in serendipity.

## Match Mode to Problem (with Substances)
**When to use**: Choosing how to attack a task.
**How**: Analytical → hyperfocus at Biological Prime Time (+ caffeine). Creative/insight → scatterfocus at Creative Prime Time (low energy; occasional drink for pure-creative). Caffeine narrows focus; alcohol widens wandering.
