# Hyperfocus — Cheatsheet

## The two modes (know which you need)
| | HYPERFOCUS | SCATTERFOCUS |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Attention **outward**, one thing | Attention **inward**, nothing in particular |
| Best mode for | **Productivity** | **Creativity** |
| Fills attentional space with | one complex task | free space to connect dots |
| Fueled by | mental energy (drains it) | rest (restores it) |
| Boost with | caffeine, Biological Prime Time | low energy, Creative Prime Time, (occasional) alcohol |

## The Four Types of Tasks — where should you be?
| | Attractive | Unattractive |
|---|---|---|
| **Productive** | ✅ **Purposeful** (sweet spot; ~3–4 tasks) | ✅ **Necessary** (push yourself) |
| **Unproductive** | ⚠️ **Distracting** (small doses) | ❌ **Unnecessary** (active laziness) |
> Live in the top row. Sort your tasks here first.

## Decision rules
- **What to focus on?** → the most **consequential** task (the domino toppling a hundred others), not the most urgent/stimulating.
- **Analytical problem?** → hyperfocus (+ caffeine). **Creative/insight problem?** → scatterfocus (low energy).
- **Am I productive?** → Did I accomplish what I **intended**? (Busy ≠ productive. A planned lazy day = productive.)
- **Should I break?** → If you're switching a lot, rereading, or drifting to mindless work — yes, and take it *before* you feel fatigued.
- **Worth consuming?** → Treat it as a "pitch" for your attention. Useful > balanced > entertaining > trashy.

## Hyperfocus in 4 stages
1. **Choose** a purposeful object → 2. **Eliminate** distractions (in advance) → 3. **Focus** for a preset time → 4. **Draw back** when you wander (you will, 47% of the day).
> Intention precedes attention. Make it an **implementation intention** (when/where/which action) → 62% vs 22% follow-through.

## Key numbers (thresholds & defaults)
- Attentional space: **~4 chunks** / ~40 conscious bits per second.
- Focus lasts **~40 seconds** at a computer before interruption.
- Never pair **two complex tasks**; only complex + habitual.
- Task-switching makes work **~50% slower** (attention residue).
- Breaks: at least every **90 min**, **~15 min per hour** worked.
- Sleep: 1 hr missed ≈ **2 hrs** lost productivity; deficit shrinks attentional space up to **60%**.
- Caffeine: benefit up to ~200mg; avoid >400mg.

## Where the wandering mind goes → why scatterfocus is productive
Past **12%** · Present **28%** · Future **48%** (prospective bias). In scatterfocus you're **14×** more likely to plan the future than when focused.

## Three scatterfocus styles
- **Capture** — roam free, write it down → clears the mind.
- **Problem-crunching** — circle one problem → for a big decision (sparingly).
- **Habitual** — simple enjoyable task + notepad → **most powerful**; recharges + connects most dots.

## Tells you need to recharge
Frequent switching · losing grip on intentions · slower pace (rereading) · drifting to mindless work · slipping into scatterfocus unintentionally.

## Master habit
**Meta-awareness** — hourly, ask "what's in my attentional space, and is it what I intended?" Meditation is the one proven way to *enlarge* that space.
