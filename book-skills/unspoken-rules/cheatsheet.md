# Cheatsheet — The Unspoken Rules

Decision rules and tells to keep beside you. The *judgment*, not the vocabulary.

## The master test: the Three Cs
Before any move, ask: **does this show competence, commitment, AND compatibility?** Each fails two ways:

| C | Undershoot (reads as…) | Overshoot (reads as…) | Aim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competence | clueless, sloppy, slow | know-it-all, makes others look bad | do it fully, accurately, promptly |
| Commitment | apathetic, clock-watcher | doormat, burning out, fake | present and eager, with boundaries |
| Compatibility | cold, hard to be around | poser, sucking up, inauthentic | comfortable to work with, still yourself |

> Managers judge the **perceived** version of your work, not just the actual output. Make progress visible.

## Should I act / ask / push? (curiosity vs. entitlement)
- Imagine getting a flat **"no."** Honest reaction "just thought I'd ask" → **ask, it's curiosity.** Reaction "outrageous!" → **don't, it's entitlement.**
- Worst case is almost always a "no," not danger. Fear is of judgment, not safety → **give it a shot.**
- Got an unearned edge? **Be less entitled.** Habitually wait for permission? **Be more.**

## Is this a good question to ask? (three circles)
- I already know it → don't ask.
- I could figure it out (Google, the wiki, 5 min) → **figure it out, don't ask.**
- Only learnable by asking → **ask** — and show your homework: *"Here's what I know, here's what I don't."*

## New here — which mode am I in?
- **Learner mode** (new): expected to *ask*. Always have a good question ready; "Any questions?" is a yes-question.
- **Leader mode** (established): expected to *know and contribute*. Don't ask what you should already own.

## How urgent / important is this task?
- **Urgency** = proximity + scrutiny + anxiety + time-sensitivity.
- **Importance** = centrality + scrutiny + criticality.
- Triage: urgent+important → **do now** · important not urgent → **schedule** · urgent not important → **timebox/delegate** · neither → **delete.**
- Force-rank ties. Default: **under-promise, over-deliver; no surprises.**

## Receiving a task — clarify before you start
1. **What / How / By When** — get all three up front.
2. **RACI** — who's Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed?
3. Work backward from the deadline; ask for **interim check-ins**.
4. **Repeat it back** to catch misunderstandings.
5. Stuck? Propose **A or B + a recommendation**, never an open "what do you think?"

## Before you hit Send (digital signals)
Typos? · Caught up on the thread? · Right time? · Tone matches intent? · **OK if this gets forwarded?**
- Match the room: **email-first vs. IM-first**; **monochronic** (time = blocks, be prompt) vs. **polychronic** (time = fluid).
- You're judged on **impact, not intent** — over-clarify in writing.

## Appearance: how much to conform
- **Mirror** coworkers at your level; unsure → default **more formal.**
- Sort each element: **Sacred** (protect) · **Negotiable** (weigh by situation) · **Indifferent** (give away freely).
- Tactic: **bend** — conform first, reveal your authentic self once trust is built.

## Reading a new team (tells)
- Power ≠ title. Watch who decisions **route through** → the influencer: Gatekeeper / Veteran / Expert / Socialite / Adviser.
- **Friendly ≠ loyal to you.** Note first loyalties before confiding.
- Swimlane rigid? → **ask permission first.** Loose? → **act, then inform.** In doubt → ask.
- Don't overshoot your **zone of commitment** — it reads as threatening.

## Decoding feedback (two axes)
| | Verbal | Nonverbal |
|---|---|---|
| **Indirect** | Subtle hints (softened) | Behavior changes (avoidance, micromanaging) |
| **Direct** | Uncensored comments (blunt) | Blunt actions (work redone/reassigned/rejected) |
- Separate **substance** (what they mean) from **delivery** (how they said it). Ask the root cause.
- Remember **negativity bias** — one criticism outweighs three compliments; don't spiral.
- Ask: *"What should I start / stop / keep doing?"* Apply fast, **report back** (coachability = commitment).

## Something's wrong — fix it / live with it / leave it
1. **Diagnose root cause**: is it **People / Position / Place?** (Use the "Why?" exercise.)
2. **Strip emotion** with **10-10-10**: best in 10 minutes / 10 months / 10 years?
3. Choose the **path of least regret**, not the fastest relief.
4. Leaning toward leaving? Run the **ten-question leave-it test** — more "yes" = more sense to go. Protect your **last impression.**

## Positioning for promotion
- **Nine-box**: aim top-right = do today's job well **AND** show you can do the next one / lead.
- Claim an **unoccupied swimlane** — what can I **do / fix / bridge / know / share** that no one owns?
- Contribution counts only if **noticed AND reported**, and **better** (not merely different), on a **mission-critical** problem.
- Asking for a raise: **data + one-page summary + a specific number + appreciation.**

## Build relationships (when you know no one)
- Answer **bids** (small connection requests) — don't let them drop.
- Can't relate? Fall back to **EAR**: Engage, Ask, Repeat. Use **"Yes, and…"**
- First move by audience: **casual chat** (nearby) / **double opt-in intro** (warm) / **customized cold email** (stranger).
- Be an **energy giver**, not a complainer. Relationships **compound** — start early.
