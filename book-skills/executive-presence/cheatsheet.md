# Executive Presence — Cheatsheet

Decision-ready rules from Hewlett. Keep beside you before a high-stakes moment.

## The Three Pillars (weight & rule)
| Pillar | "How you…" | Exec weight | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gravitas** | act | 67% (core) | Fix first — without it, no title makes you a leader |
| **Communication** | speak | 28% | It's *how* not *what*; content persuades only ~15% |
| **Appearance** | look | 5% | The *filter* — fail it and no one assesses the rest |

**Rule of thumb**: Don't aim for straight A's. Nail ~3 top picks in *each* pillar to your natural strengths and avoid serious blunders.

## Decision Rules
- **When merit isn't translating into advancement** → audit all three pillars; the gap is almost always EP, not competence.
- **When in a crisis** → own the mistake publicly and fast; handling catastrophe > avoiding it. Never deflect blame.
- **When a decision is being avoided** → render *a* decision (not necessarily the perfect one) when no one else dares — but only after listening and finding the timely opening.
- **When you must show teeth as a woman/minority** → frame it around the team/company; take the "I" out.
- **When speaking truth to power** → come from a core of values (not grievance); leaven with humor to widen receptivity.
- **When opening a talk** → connect in 5 seconds with a short personal story; then lose the props and make eye contact.
- **When you have a point in a meeting** → snipe, don't spray: wait for a strong counterpoint or insightful question. But if *you're* leading, open with a bold agenda-setting statement.
- **When dressing** → "appropriate for your environment, authentic to you." Dress for the job you want.
- **When told "you're doing great"** → distrust it; ask for specific/timely/prescriptive feedback on a named recent encounter.
- **When given vague criticism** ("be more vulnerable") → ask "How is what I'm doing getting in the way of my job?" It's usually the culture, not you.
- **When you hit the fork (conformity costs your identity)** → distinguish "playing the game" from "selling out." Only you draw the line; don't put your soul in play.
- **When a culture violates a non-negotiable** → walk away. "Nothing is worth that."
- **When you feel slighted** → assume ignorance before bigotry; engage rather than withdraw.

## Voice & Delivery Thresholds
- Optimal voice frequency ≈ **125 Hz**; lower reads as smart/successful. (−22 Hz ≈ +$187K CEO comp.)
- Persuasion mix: **passion 27% · voice 23% · presence 15% · content 15%.**
- Judgments of competence/likability/trust form in **250 milliseconds.**
- Kill: uptalk, "um/like/you know," shrillness, breathlessness, list-reading, phone-checking.
- Use: pauses/silence, slowed pace, "phrasing off," narrative over bullet points, succinctness.

## The Great Feedback Formula
**Timely + Specific + Prescriptive + Business-framed.** After it, the receiver knows *what* the problem is, *why* it matters, *exactly what to do*.
- Give: frequent discrete pointers · never while angry (wait 24h) · good things first · embed correctives · catch people getting it right · "I want you to be successful."
- Get: request it on a named encounter · thick skin · coach if needed · peer circle · sponsor · don't cry/burn bridges.

## Six Gravitas Behaviors (self-audit)
Confidence under fire (79/76%) · Decisiveness (70%) · Integrity/truth-to-power (60%+) · EQ (~60%) · Reputation (56/57%) · Vision (50–54%).

## Six Communication Traits
Superior speaking · Command a room · Assertiveness · Read the room · Humor/banter · Body language & posture.

## Five Appearance Aspects
Polish & grooming (top) · Fit/wellness (not thinness) · Stylish clothes for your next job · Height (compensable) · Youth/vigor. **Golden rule: minimize distractions from your competence.**

## Blunder Watch (career-enders in bold)
- **Gravitas**: **lack of integrity**, **sexual impropriety**, bullying, lack of depth.
- **Communication**: crying, phone-checking, long-windedness, notes/props, breathlessness.
- **Appearance**: provocative dress (women), disheveled/unkempt (men), bling that steals thunder.

## The Double Binds (tightrope — recognize, don't internalize)
| Bind | "Too much" | "Too little" |
|---|---|---|
| Force | bossy / bitch (31%) | passive (31%) |
| Horn | tooting (29%) | self-deprecating (24%) |
| Warmth | too nice | no empathy |
| Voice | shrill | tentative |
| Age (women) | too old | too young (sweet spot 39–42) |

Contradictory feedback = hidden bias, not your defect. **Widen latitude**: team-framed arguments, humor, sniping, a bold brand, credentials, visible care.
