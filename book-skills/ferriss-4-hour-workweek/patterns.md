# Patterns & Techniques — The 4-Hour Work Week

## Fear-Setting (the 7 questions)
**When to use**: any big, scary decision you keep postponing.
**How**: in writing — (1) Define the nightmare in detail; rate permanence/probability 1–10. (2) Steps to repair the damage. (3) Benefits of more-probable outcomes (rate 1–10). (4) If fired today, how to regain financial control. (5) What you're putting off out of fear. (6) Cost of inaction over 1/5/10 years. (7) What are you waiting for?
**Trade-offs**: takes an hour and forces honesty; defuses fear but won't remove real (rare) irreversible risks — those you keep.

## Dreamlining → TMI/TDI
**When to use**: to convert "I want freedom" into a concrete income target.
**How**: 6- and 12-month timelines × (Have / Be / Do), 5 each; convert "Be" → "Do"; star top 4; **TMI = (4 dream costs + monthly expenses) × 1.3**; **TDI = TMI ÷ 30**.
**Trade-offs**: monthly cash-flow framing makes dreams look affordable, but requires honest expense accounting.

## The 80/20 Two-Question Audit
**When to use**: every 2–4 weeks, on business and personal life.
**How**: (1) Which 20% of sources cause 80% of problems/unhappiness? → eliminate/fire. (2) Which 20% produce 80% of results/happiness? → double down.
**Trade-offs**: firing the draining 20% can feel risky; usually the revenue/energy math strongly favors it.

## Parkinson's Law (short deadlines)
**When to use**: any task that expands to fill available time.
**How**: assign an aggressively short deadline; force execution of essentials only. Pair with 80/20 (limit tasks *and* shorten time).
**Trade-offs**: quality is usually equal or better; occasionally too tight — recalibrate, don't abandon.

## The Low-Information Diet
**When to use**: overwhelm, news anxiety, chronic "researching."
**How**: one-week total media fast (cold turkey) → rebuild only just-in-time sources → ask "will I use this immediately and importantly?" → practice nonfinishing.
**Trade-offs**: you'll miss some conversations; you lose almost nothing actionable.

## Batching + the Batch Cost/Benefit Test
**When to use**: recurring time-consumer tasks (email, calls, mail, reports).
**How**: pick a frequency; compute (hours saved × your $/hr) − (cost of problems caused by the delay). If net positive, batch further apart; if negative, step back one frequency (weekly → twice-weekly, not daily).
**Trade-offs**: slower responses; large gains in deep-work time; risk capped (Ferriss: nothing cost >$300 to fix).

## Empowerment via If-Then Rules
**When to use**: to stop being a decision bottleneck.
**How**: give outsourcers/co-workers written rules + a spending limit (e.g., "fix anything under $400 without asking"); review monthly and add new rules from their best decisions.
**Trade-offs**: occasional small losses; huge time freedom and a self-improving system.

## The Puppy Dog Close
**When to use**: overcoming resistance to a permanent change (remote work, no-meeting policy, autonomy).
**How**: propose a small, reversible "let's just try it once" trial; the return rarely happens.
**Trade-offs**: slower than demanding the change; far higher success rate.

## Eliminate → Automate → Delegate
**When to use**: before assigning any task to a VA or system.
**How**: cut the task; if it survives, systematize it; only then delegate what remains. Assign VAs one task at a time (two max), each with a clear deliverable.
**Trade-offs**: more upfront thinking; prevents paying to preserve worthless work.

## Muse Design (product + market)
**When to use**: building automated income to hit your TMI.
**How**: (1) niche market first (magazine full-page ad <$5,000; ≥15,000 readers); (2) product meeting 4 criteria — one-sentence benefit, $50–200, ≤3–4 wk to make, FAQ-able; (3) source via resell < license < create (info products ideal); control distribution, hold premium pricing.
**Trade-offs**: narrow markets cap size but cut competition and cost, and command premium prices.

## Micro-Testing (validate before manufacturing)
**When to use**: before spending on inventory.
**How**: 1–3 page test site → Google AdWords PPC (~$500, ~5 days, many narrow keywords > few broad) → "temporarily out of stock" page captures purchase intent without charging → read conversion. Or probe on eBay first. Manufacture only if it converts.
**Trade-offs**: costs ~$500 and a few days; saves months and inventory risk.

## Virtual Architecture (Management by Absence)
**When to use**: after a validated muse, to remove yourself from operations.
**How**: 3-phase rollout — DIY (build FAQ from real questions) → local fulfillment (net-30) → end-to-end fulfillment + call center + processor that talk to each other. Use replaceable contract *companies*, not freelancers; check reports weekly/monthly, banks 1st & 15th.
**Trade-offs**: setup fees and coordination; buys near-total absence and scalability.

## The Hourglass Remote-Work Escape (5 steps)
**When to use**: employees wanting location freedom (DELA).
**How**: (1) raise your value + get invested-in; (2) prove remote productivity covertly with records; (3) build a business-case bullet list; (4) propose a small revocable trial (bracket: ask 2, accept 1); (5) expand stepwise to full-time; keep a funded willingness to quit as leverage.
**Trade-offs**: takes ~2 months and nerve; if refused, the job is "beyond repair" — leave.
