# Cheatsheet — The 4-Hour Work Week

## Decision rules (if X, do Y, because Z)
- **If you're avoiding a decision "until the timing is right"** → run fear-setting now, because the only real answer is fear and inaction is the bigger risk.
- **If a task feels important but isn't moving you toward a goal** → eliminate it, because efficiency at the wrong task is worthless (effectiveness > efficiency).
- **If a task keeps expanding** → give it a hard, short deadline, because work fills the time allotted (Parkinson).
- **If a recurring task interrupts you** → batch it on a schedule, because setup/switching cost is fixed (~45 min to resume).
- **If someone needs your OK for something small** → give a written if-then rule + spending limit, because that's an empowerment failure, not real management.
- **If you want a permanent change accepted** → propose a small reversible trial (Puppy Dog Close), because people resist permanence but accept "just once."
- **If you're about to delegate** → check you can't eliminate or automate it first, because you'd be paying to preserve waste.
- **If you have a product idea** → find the market first, then micro-test before manufacturing, because filling demand beats creating it and behavior beats opinion.
- **If cash flow already funds your dreams** → stop chasing more customers, because that's W4W.
- **If you feel overwhelmed** → take a break and 80/20 both business and life, because lack of time is lack of priorities.

## DEAL — the sequence (and DELA for employees)
| Step | What | Key tools |
|---|---|---|
| **D**efinition | rewrite the rules, set the target | New Rich rules, fear-setting, dreamlining/TMI |
| **E**limination | buy time | effectiveness, 80/20, Parkinson, low-info diet, batching |
| **A**utomation | buy income | eliminate→automate→delegate, VAs, muse, MBA architecture |
| **L**iberation | buy mobility | hourglass remote escape, mini-retirements |
> Employees implement as **DELA**: secure location freedom *before* cutting hours.

## Tells & smells (you're in trouble if…)
- You check email first thing / last thing / constantly → resets the whole day.
- You're the tollbooth every decision passes through → build rules and route around yourself.
- You're solving the same problem twice → write an if-then rule.
- You're making non-urgent things urgent, or blowing up minutiae → W4W relapse.
- You feel busy but can't name the 20% that matters → skipped your 80/20 review.
- You're "researching" with no decision pending → just-in-case information; stop.
- You work where you live/sleep/relax → separate environments.

## The Not-to-Do List (9)
1 Don't answer calls from unknown numbers · 2 Don't email first/last thing · 3 No meetings/calls without a clear agenda + end time · 4 Don't let people ramble ("how can I help you?") · 5 Don't check email constantly — batch · 6 Don't over-serve low-profit, high-maintenance customers · 7 Don't work more to fix overwhelm — prioritize · 8 Don't carry a phone 24/7 · 9 Don't expect work to fill a void relationships/activities should.

## Refusal scripts
- Invader at your desk: **"I'm right in the middle of something. How can I help you?"** (unclear in 30s → "send me a quick email to remind me.")
- Time limit: **"OK, I've got two minutes before a call — what's the situation and how can I help?"**
- Replace **"How are you?"** with **"How can I help you?"** — get specific, no stories.
