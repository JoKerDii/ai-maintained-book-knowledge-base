## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The premise.** You don't want $1M — you want the *freedom* it supposedly buys, and that freedom is buyable now by separating income from time. The measure of wealth is **relative income** ($/hr of life), not absolute income ($/yr). Money's value is multiplied by the **freedom multiplier**: control of the four W's — *what* you do, *when*, *where*, *with whom*.

**DEAL — the master process** (the book's spine):
- **D — Definition**: rewrite the rules. Adopt the New Rich mindset, clear fear with **fear-setting**, and set a concrete target with **dreamlining → Target Monthly Income (TMI)**.
- **E — Elimination**: buy time. **Effectiveness > efficiency**; apply **80/20** and **Parkinson's Law** together; go on a **low-information diet**; **batch** tasks and master the **art of refusal**.
- **A — Automation**: buy income. **Eliminate before you delegate**; use **virtual assistants**; build a **muse** (automated business) and run it by **Management by Absence**.
- **L — Liberation**: buy mobility. Use the **hourglass approach** to escape the office; live via **mini-retirements**.
> **Employees implement as DELA** — win location freedom *before* cutting hours, or a boss will block you.

**The 10 New Rich Rules** (decision filters): 1 Retirement = worst-case insurance · 2 Energy is cyclical → mini-retirements · 3 Less ≠ laziness (be productive, not busy) · 4 Timing is never right · 5 Ask forgiveness, not permission · 6 Emphasize strengths, don't fix weaknesses · 7 Excess becomes its opposite · 8 Money alone isn't the solution · 9 Relative > absolute income · 10 Kill **distress**, seek **eustress**.

**Fear-Setting** (Ch 3): defeat paralysis by writing the worst case in detail (rate permanence/probability 1–10 — most disasters are ~3–4 and repairable), then measuring the **cost of inaction** over 1/5/10 years. *Redefine risk = likelihood of an irreversible negative outcome → inaction is usually the biggest risk.*

**Dreamlining → TMI** (Ch 4): fill 6- & 12-month timelines with Have/Be/Do (convert "Be" → "Do"), star the top 4, then **TMI = (4 dream costs + monthly expenses) × 1.3**; **TDI = TMI ÷ 30**. Freedom becomes a cash-flow number your muse must hit.

**Elimination engine** (Ch 5–7): **80/20** — which 20% causes 80% of problems (cut) and which produces 80% of results (multiply), reviewed every 2–4 weeks. **Parkinson** — short deadlines force focus. **Low-information diet** — consume only just-in-time, immediately actionable info (start with a one-week media fast). **Batching** + **if-then rules** + the **Puppy Dog Close** (win change via a small reversible trial).

**Muse-building** (Ch 8–11): **Eliminate → Automate → Delegate.** Find the **niche market first**, then a product meeting 4 criteria (one-sentence benefit, **$50–200**, ≤3–4 wk to make, FAQ-able); source via resell < license < **create** (info products ideal). **Micro-test** with ~$500 of AdWords before manufacturing. Then **Management by Absence**: a self-correcting virtual architecture — "be the roadside cop, not the tollbooth."

**Liberation** (Ch 12–13): the **hourglass remote escape** (long proof-of-concept → short trial → negotiate up), **mini-retirements** funded by **geoarbitrage**, and **filling the void** with continual learning + service. Beware the **Top 13 mistakes** — mostly W4W and micromanagement relapses.
