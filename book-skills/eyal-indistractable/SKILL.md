## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The central reframe**: The opposite of distraction is not focus — it's **traction**. Both come from Latin *trahere* ("to pull"). **Traction** = any action pulling you *toward* what you planned. **Distraction** = any action pulling you *away*. You cannot call something a distraction unless you know what it's distracting you *from*. Every action is prompted by an **internal trigger** (from within: boredom, anxiety) or an **external trigger** (environment: pings, people).

### The 4-Part Indistractable Model (apply in order)
1. **Master Internal Triggers** — Distraction is an escape from discomfort, so *time management is pain management*. Devices are the *proximate* cause; discomfort is the *root* cause. Cope, don't suppress (suppression rebounds — the "white bear"). Tools: **reimagine the trigger** (look → write down → explore with curiosity → beware liminal moments), the **10-minute rule / surf the urge**, **reimagine the task** (hunt for novelty; play holds attention), **reimagine your temperament** (willpower isn't a finite tank — ego depletion is largely debunked; use self-compassion, drop self-limiting labels).
2. **Make Time for Traction** — Turn values into time across the **three life domains** (You, Relationships, Work). **Timebox** the whole week (implementation intentions; eliminate white space) — success = *did you do what you planned*, not what you did. **Reflect & Refine** weekly (15 min). **Control the inputs, not the outcomes** (you control showing up, not results). **Sync your schedule** with stakeholders.
3. **Hack Back External Triggers** — For each, ask the **Critical Question**: *"Is this trigger serving me, or am I serving it?"* Behavior needs **B = MAT** (Motivation + Ability + Trigger) — remove the trigger and it can't fire. Hack back work interruptions (signal "don't interrupt"), **email** (*TNT: T = n × t* — send fewer, batch, tag by reply-time), meetings ("no agenda, no meeting"), the **smartphone** (*4 R's: Remove, Replace, Rearrange, Reclaim*), desktop, articles (Pocket + temptation bundling), and feeds (kill the feed).
4. **Prevent Distraction with Pacts** — A **precommitment** (Ulysses pact) binds your future self. **Effort pact** (make it harder — Forest, Focusmate, kSafe), **price pact** (money on the line — loss aversion; "burn or burn"), **identity pact** (become "indistractable"; say "I don't," not "I can't"). *Use pacts only after the first three steps.*

### Applying it beyond yourself
- **Workplace**: Chronic tech-overuse is a *symptom* of low **psychological safety** + high job strain (high expectations × low control). Fix the culture (start small, open a safe forum), not just the tech.
- **Children**: Overuse signals unmet **psychological nutrients** — Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness (self-determination theory). Meet these offline; set limits *with* kids; let them own their pacts.
- **Relationships**: Distraction is contagious — build **social antibodies** against phubbing ("I see you're on your phone. Is everything OK?"); apply all four steps to protect intimacy.

**Identity default**: Call yourself *indistractable* — striving to do what you say you will do. "Strive" means struggle, not perfection.
