# Cheatsheet — In the Line of Fire

## The one rule above all
**Question → Buffer → Answer (head-on, ≤60s, with evidence) → Topspin.** Skip the answer = evasive (politician's sin). Skip the Topspin = no influence (businessperson's sin). And: **every answer must be true.**

## Decision tree: a question just landed
- **Do I understand it 100%?** No → **Return to Sender** ("I'm sorry, I didn't follow; would you mind restating?"). Never interpret/speculate.
- **Is it factually inaccurate / an accusation?** → Skip the Buffer; **refute immediately** ("No."), then support, then restate rebuttal.
- **Is the charge actually true?** → **Guilty-as-Charged**: Buffer → Agree → "But…" → Evidence → Call to action+benefit.
- **Multiple unrelated questions?** → Pick ONE; answer; then "You had another question."
- **Otherwise** → Buffer (paraphrase or key word) → wait for head nod → answer → Topspin.

## Buffer: pick your form
| Form | When | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Paraphrase** ("Why this price point?") | hostile question; need thinking time | overuse = stilted |
| **"You" + paraphrase** ("Your question is…") | want Eye Connect + read the nod | sounds like a "hoot owl" if every time |
| **Key Words** ("Our pricing rationale is…") | Roman Column already crystal clear | zero thinking time — must be sure |

## Cool-zone rule (positioning the Roman Column)
- **Repeat** the charged question → land in **"Guilty!"** (left danger zone).
- **Reverse/invalidate** it → land in **"Contentious!"** (right danger zone).
- **Neutral paraphrase of the bare key word** → **cool zone**. ✅

## Find the Roman Column — don't grab the wrapper
| Hostile question ends with… | Wrapper (wrong) | Roman Column (right) |
|---|---|---|
| "…charging so much!" | overpricing/expensive | **price** |
| "…think you can survive?" | survive | **compete** |
| "…you, a woman…old boy network?" | sexism | **capability** |
| "…throw in your lot / get acquired?" | merge | **independence** |

## Tells & smells (read the room)
- **Involuntary head nod** after your Buffer = you nailed the Roman Column → answer now. **Frown / head-shake** = wrong → Return to Sender.
- Questioner's voice rises on **"really"** / **"saying"** ("What I'm *really* asking…") = "You're not listening!" alarm.
- Your own **slouch / hands-in-pockets / glance at watch** = audience reads "bored, doesn't care" within ~5 seconds.
- On camera, **involuntary disdain** (Scowl/Sigh) is fatal — assume you're always on a split screen.

## Three ways to lose (diagnose fast)
**Defensive** (squirm/stammer) · **Evasive** (answer a different question) · **Contentious** (attack — the worst; = visible loss of control). All are Fight-or-Flight. Antidote: firm, calm resolve + the sequence.

## Delete from your vocabulary
"Let me see if I have this right…" · "Could you repeat the question?" · "Does that answer your question?" · "Is what you're asking…?" · "The question is…" · "What you're *really* asking…" · "If I understand your question…" · "The issue/concern is…" · "As I said…" · "Why do you ask?"

## Special questions — fast rules
- **Unknown** → admit, promise follow-up, ask for a card (unless central — then answer).
- **Confidential** → give a positive *reason* (policy/security/legality); never "I can't reveal that." Then Topspin.
- **Speculative/forecast** → "It's impossible to say how long it'll take, **but**…" → Topspin to Point B + WIIFY.
- **Irrelevant** → there's no such thing; Buffer the snicker away, then answer or take offline.

## Topspin targets
**Point B** = what you want them to DO (call to action). **WIIFY** = WHY they should (their benefit). End on one or both — never on a negative. Earn it by answering first.

## Prep checklist (before the session)
☐ List the questions you *don't* want to hear ☐ Position statement (press-release style) for each red-flag issue (Mgmt, Competition, Growth, Price, Contingencies, Timing, Problems, IP) ☐ Gather evidence ☐ **Verbalize aloud** Buffers + answers + a Topspin on each, with colleagues firing questions ☐ Set time expectations; plan the countdown.

## Open-the-floor mechanics
Open hand, **never point** (one-plus-three). All names **or** no names — never selective. Raise your hand to invite hands — but **not** with investors/board.
