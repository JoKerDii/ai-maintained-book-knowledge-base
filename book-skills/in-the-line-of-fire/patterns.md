# Patterns & Techniques — In the Line of Fire

## The Winning Sequence (master pattern)
**When to use**: every challenging question.
**How**: Question → **Buffer** (neutralize) → **Answer** the Roman Column head-on, with evidence → **Topspin** (Point B / WIIFY).
**Trade-offs**: Politicians skip the answer (jump to Topspin) → look evasive. Businesspeople skip the Topspin → exert zero influence. Do all four.

## Find the Roman Column
**When**: before answering anything.
**How**: "Empty your cup"; listen for the 1–2 nouns/verbs that are the essence; sub-vocalize them ("She's worried about cost"). Strip away emotion, misinformation, ramble. Watch for a *double* column.
**Trade-offs**: Mistaking the emotional wrapper (e.g., "sexism," "survive") for the column lands you in a danger zone.

## Paraphrase Buffer
**When**: hostile/charged questions; buys thinking time.
**How**: Interrogative (What/Why/How/Does/Can/Is) + Roman Column, voice drops at end. Strip charged words; fewest words possible. Keep it in the **cool zone** (don't repeat = guilty; don't reverse = contentious).
**Trade-offs**: Overuse sounds stilted ("hoot owl"), especially "you"-phrased.

## Key Word Buffer (advanced)
**When**: you already have 100% clarity; non-challenging direct questions; when you want to look razor-sharp.
**How**: embed the key word and roll straight into the answer ("Our pricing rationale is…").
**Trade-offs**: zero thinking time — only safe when the Roman Column is crystal clear. (Powell, Schwarzkopf.)

## The Power of "You"
**When**: building a Double Buffer you'd otherwise delete.
**How**: "Your question is…" not "The question is…"; keeps Eye Connect, reads the head nod, most persuasive word in English.

## Triple Fail-Safe
**When**: every answer, as a guard against rushing.
**How**: ① Don't retake the floor without the Roman Column → Return to Sender. ② Buffer with the key word; hold Eye Connect until the involuntary head nod. ③ Frown instead of nod → Return to Sender.

## Return to Sender
**When**: you don't understand 100% (not 99.999%), or you get a frown.
**How**: take responsibility — "I'm sorry, I didn't follow; would you mind restating the question?" Never interpret, speculate, or use the deafness ruse.

## Guilty-as-Charged Sequence (5 steps)
**When**: the charge is *true*.
**How**: **Buffer → Agree** (their words) **→ "But…"** (loud; don't agree too long) **→ Evidence → Call to action + benefit** (Point B + WIIFY).
**Trade-offs**: In *media* exchanges, do NOT agree (sound-bite risk) — counter immediately or bury the admission in a subordinate clause before Topspin.

## Refute the Inaccurate Question
**When**: a question contains/implies a falsehood ("When did you stop beating your wife?").
**How**: skip the Buffer; immediate complete refutation — "No." Then support, then restate the rebuttal. (Powell: "No, of course not… But there is no list.")

## Handle Special Question Types
- **Multiple disparate**: pick ONE (easiest/hardest/last/surprising), Buffer + answer it, then "You had another question."
- **Statement (non-question)**: turn into a question via paraphrase.
- **Irrelevant/Tangential**: no such thing as irrelevant — Buffer to suppress the snicker, then answer or take offline.
- **Unknown**: admit it, promise follow-up, ask for a card (unless central — then you must answer).
- **Confidential/Speculative**: give a positive *reason* (policy/security/legality); don't forecast; still pivot to Topspin.
- **Presented material**: answer fresh, forward references only, never "As I said…"; be succinct.

## Topspin
**When**: after a real answer, to close strong.
**How**: end with Point B (what you want them to do) and/or WIIFY (why they should). Add multiple upward swirls. Counters any "too-anything" charge (Yang to Yin).
**Trade-offs**: Topspin *before* answering = evasive (G.W. Bush / Lisa Kee). Never end on a negative (Dukakis).

## Manage the Time
**When**: opening any Q&A.
**How**: set expectations up front ("time for only a few questions"); count down at the end ("two more… last question"). (Schwarzkopf: 10 questions in 2:48.)

## Q&A Cycle Control Moves
Open floor (control time + traffic; open hand, never point; all-names-or-no-names) → Recognize → Yield (mind your body language — no slouch) → Retake (Buffer) → Answer (+Topspin). Repeats per questioner.

## Prepare & Verbalize
**When**: before any mission-critical session.
**How**: list worst questions → position statement (press-release style) per red-flag issue → enlist colleagues to fire questions → **verbalize aloud** your Buffers, answers, and a Topspin on each, repeatedly. Treat every Q&A like an IPO road show.
