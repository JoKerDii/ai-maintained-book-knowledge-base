# Chapter 10: The Role Model — Complete Control

## Core Idea
General Norman Schwarzkopf's Gulf War press conferences are the master class: in sessions of just minutes he managed time, listened, found the Roman Column, Buffered with key words, answered succinctly, and Topspinned — over and over. The **winning sequence** is Question → Buffer → Answer (with evidence) → Topspin.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **The Winning Sequence (the whole book in four beats)**:
  1. **Question** 2. **Buffer** (paraphrase or key words — self-defense) 3. **Answer** with supporting evidence (the Yang to the question's Yin) 4. **Topspin** (to Point B and/or WIIFY — agile switch to offense).
- **Controlled vs. Conventional Q&A dynamics (Figs 10.1–10.2)**: Conventional = question-dart down, answer-dart down, no control, no value. Controlled = two *upward* triangles (Buffer before, Topspin after) bracketing the answer — adding control and shifting direction in your favor.
- **The doubled challenge / doubled reward**: you must both *overcome the Fight-or-Flight instinct* and *learn the new skills* — and the payoff is also doubled: **survive and thrive without fighting.**

## Key Concepts
- **Manage the time**: Schwarzkopf set expectations ("a very few questions"), ran 10 questions in 2:48, counted down ("About two more questions… one more… last question"), and refused follow-ons (a privilege you don't have).
- **Key Word Buffer in action**: like Powell, he never used a Double Buffer or paraphrase — he rolled the question's key words straight into each answer ("chemical or biological weapons… those reports have been bogus").
- **Confidential handled gracefully**: "I'm not going to in any way discuss the location of any of the forces" — then still Topspin to Point B ("get them out of Kuwait and enforce the UN resolutions").
- **Succinctness**: "All of the above." / "Some." / "We will go around, over, through, on top, underneath, and any other way to get through." Topspin even in one line.
- **Schwarzkopf's caveats for *you***: he was the *solicited* party (you're the solicitor); his info was secret (yours must be open); he owed *no* WIIFY (you owe many). Adapt the role model to your obligations.

## Mental Models
- Picture the two gray upward triangles: never let a question-dart and answer-dart just collide; always insert a Buffer before and a Topspin after.
- "It's impossible to say how long it's going to take" — a legitimate non-answer that still pivots to Topspin; use for speculative/forecast questions.

## Worked Example
**The forecast deflection, business-translated**: Asked "How long until you release the next version?" → Buffer with the Roman Column (time) + candid non-forecast: *"It's impossible to say how long it's going to take, but I can tell you that when the next version is released, it will have the same high quality as all the products in our powerful pipeline and produce the same rich benefits to our customers."* — Schwarzkopf's exact move, repurposed: decline to forecast, then Topspin to Point B + WIIFY.

## Key Takeaways
1. The complete sequence: Question → Buffer → Answer+evidence → Topspin.
2. Manage the time: set expectations, count down, end clean.
3. Master the Key Word Buffer for sharp, no-hesitation control.
4. Even confidential/forecast questions can pivot to Topspin.
5. Never skip the answer (politicians' sin) and never skip the Topspin (businesspeople's sin).
6. Final WIIFY of the book: learn all the steps and you survive *and* thrive without fighting.
