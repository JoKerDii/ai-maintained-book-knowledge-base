# Glossary — Until the End of Time

**Adaptation vs. byproduct (spandrel)** — Whether a trait was selected for its own effects or came bundled free with something else that was selected for (Ch 6, 7, 8).

**Aperiodic crystal** — Schrödinger's prescient term for the information-bearing molecule of heredity (Ch 4).

**ATP** — The strained-spring molecule synthesized by membrane turbines that ferries energy through the cell (Ch 4).

**Attention schema** — Graziano's proposal that consciousness is the brain's radically simplified model of its own attention (Ch 5).

**Belief calculus** — The mixed reasoning-plus-emotion-plus-authority process by which beliefs actually form, mostly below awareness (Ch 7).

**Big bounce / cyclic cosmology** — Steinhardt–Turok–Ijjas alternative in which expansion dilutes entropy enough for cycles of hundreds of billions of years to repeat (Ch 10).

**Big crunch** — Collapse following a tunneling of dark energy to a negative value (Ch 10).

**Big rip** — Scenario where dark energy strengthens until repulsive gravity tears apart galaxies, stars, molecules, and atoms (Ch 9).

**Black hole evaporation** — Slow shrinkage via Hawking radiation, once a hole's temperature exceeds the ambient temperature of space (Ch 10).

**Boltzmann brain** — A free-floating mind assembled by a rare spontaneous entropy drop, complete with false memories (Ch 10).

**"Cheesecake for the mind"** — Pinker's claim that the arts are adaptively useless confections pressing pleasure buttons evolved for other purposes (Ch 8).

**CMB (afterglow of creation)** — The big bang's residual heat, now 2.7 K, with hot/cold spots differing by ~1 part in 100,000 (Ch 3).

**Cosmic horizon** — Boundary beyond which objects recede faster than light; it glows at a tiny constant temperature (Ch 9, 10).

**Costly signaling** — Extravagant, hard-to-fake investment that certifies commitment or quality (Ch 7, 8).

**Dark energy** — Uniformly spread energy whose gravity is repulsive, driving accelerated expansion (Ch 9).

**Denial of death** — Becker's thesis that mortality awareness drives literal and symbolic immortality systems (Ch 1, 7, 8).

**Dissipative adaptation** — England's proposal that driven particle collections spontaneously reconfigure to absorb and dissipate energy more efficiently (Ch 4).

**Dissipative structures** — Prigogine's configurations that spontaneously order under sustained energy flow (Ch 4).

**Eliminativism** — The position that consciousness is an illusion; **idealism** is its mirror (Ch 5).

**Emergence** — The appearance of genuinely new descriptive levels — entropy, life, mind — from coordinated simple constituents (Ch 5).

**Empire State Building timeline** — Cosmic time mapped onto floors, where floor *n* = 10^n years since the big bang (Ch 9, 10, 11).

**Entropic two-step** — Any process in which a system's entropy decreases because it exports a *more than compensating* increase to its environment (Ch 2, 3, 4, 9).

**Entropy** — A count of how many microscopic rearrangements yield the same macroscopic configuration; high = common, low = special (Ch 2, 3).

**Erasure cost** — Computation can be heat-free, but erasing memory necessarily produces waste heat — new thoughts, not thoughts per se, cost entropy (Ch 9).

**Event horizon** — A black hole's boundary of no return, radius proportional to mass (Ch 10).

**Fitness indicator** — A display whose expense certifies the quality it advertises (Ch 8).

**FOXP2** — Gene implicated in speech and grammar; identical in Neanderthals (Ch 6).

**Free will vs. human freedom** — Volitional intercession in physical law (rejected) vs. an emancipated range of response (affirmed) (Ch 5, 11).

**Hard problem of consciousness** — Chalmers' question of why physical processing is accompanied by subjective experience at all (Ch 5).

**Hawking radiation** — Particles emitted at a horizon when quantum-fluctuation pairs are split, causing the hole to lose mass (Ch 10).

**Higgs field** — Space-filling field (value 246) giving particles mass; possibly metastable (Ch 10).

**Hyperactive agency detection** — Evolved bias toward over-attributing agency; cheap false positives beat costly false negatives (Ch 6, 7).

**Inflaton field** — The "cosmic fuel" whose uniform energy produces repulsive gravity (Ch 3).

**Integrated information theory (IIT)** — Tononi and Koch's proposal that consciousness is highly integrated, highly differentiated information, quantified by ф (Ch 5).

**Macrostate** — The set of microscopic configurations sharing the same macroscopic properties; entropy counts its members (Ch 2).

**Meaning as created, not found** — Greene's conclusion that value and purpose are invented inside subjective worlds, constrained but not dictated by evolved predispositions (Ch 11).

**Milkomeda** — The merged Milky Way–Andromeda galaxy (Ch 9).

**Minimally counterintuitive concept** — An idea violating one or two intuitive expectations, making it memorable and transmissible (Ch 6, 7).

**Molecular Darwinism** — Natural selection operating on replicating molecules before life exists (Ch 1, 4).

**Monomyth** — Campbell's template: call, perilous adventure, return transformed (Ch 6).

**Multiverse** — If space is infinite, a patchwork of ~90-billion-light-year regions in which every history recurs infinitely often (Ch 10).

**Negative entropy** — Schrödinger's term for the low-entropy structure organisms ingest to hold their own entropy down (Ch 4).

**Nested stories (reductionism without eliminativism)** — Multiple level-specific accounts of one reality; consistency across levels is required, replacement is not possible (Ch 1, 4, 5).

**Past hypothesis** — The postulate that the universe began in an extraordinarily low-entropy state (Ch 2, 3).

**Panpsychism / proto-consciousness** — Chalmers' revival of an irreducible primitive subjective quality in matter (Ch 5).

**Physicalism** — The view that conscious experience will be fully explained by physical properties and existing physical law (Ch 5).

**Proton decay** — Predicted disintegration of protons; not yet observed, lower bound ~10^34 years (Ch 9).

**Proximate vs. ultimate explanation** — "It feels wonderful" answers the immediate impetus only, not the evolutionary origin (Ch 8).

**Qualia** — The subjective felt characters of experience (Ch 5).

**Redox reaction** — Electron transfer; life's universal slow burn, staged in small steps (Ch 4).

**Repulsive gravity** — Outward-pushing gravity arising when energy is spread uniformly rather than clumped (Ch 3, 9).

**RNA world** — Hypothesis exploiting RNA's dual role as software and hardware to bypass the origin-of-life chicken-and-egg (Ch 4).

**Sacred, the** — The status conferred on certain stories and rules that elevates them above all others (Ch 7).

**Scheffler's afterlife scenario** — Thought experiment removing descendants rather than yourself, isolating what anchors value (Ch 11).

**Second law of thermodynamics** — Total entropy of a system plus its environment overwhelmingly likely to increase (Ch 2, 3).

**SPONCH** — Sulfur, phosphorus, oxygen, nitrogen, carbon, hydrogen — the atoms dominating all known life (Ch 4).

**Story as flight simulator** — Oatley's refinement of Pinker: fiction as safe, cheap rehearsal that refines intuition (Ch 6).

**Sublime, the** — Experience so enveloping it feels like stepping beyond time (Ch 8).

**Substrate independence of mind** — Treating life and mind as processes rather than as cells and brains (Ch 9).

**Symbolic immortality** — Rank's displacement of the urge for eternity onto self-authored works (Ch 1, 8).

**Terror management theory** — Mortality salience covertly amplifies defense of one's cultural worldview (Ch 7).

**Theory of mind** — Attribution of beliefs, desires, and intentions to other agents and to oneself (Ch 5, 6, 7).

**Universal grammar** — Chomsky's innate neurobiological language primer (Ch 6).

**Vacuum decay** — Higgs tunneling to a new value, spreading near light speed and rewriting physics inside the bubble (Ch 10).

**Worm at the core** — James's term for tamped-down awareness of one's own ending underlying ordinary delight (Ch 1).
