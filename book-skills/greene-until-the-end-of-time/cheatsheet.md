# Cheatsheet — Until the End of Time

## The Cosmic-Era Timeline (Empire State Building: floor *n* = 10^n years after the bang)

| Floor | Years | What ends / what happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1–9 | 10–10⁹ | Particles, atoms, stars, planets, life, mind. **We are on floor ~10.** |
| 10 | 10¹⁰ | Present epoch. Sun dies ~5 Gyr; Milky Way + Andromeda → Milkomeda |
| 11 | 10¹¹ | Distant galaxies pass the cosmic horizon; the observable sky empties |
| 12–14 | 10¹²–10¹⁴ | Era of red dwarfs; last star formation ends; **traditional life/mind window closes** |
| 15–20 | 10¹⁵–10²⁰ | Burnt-out stars and wandering planets; solar systems unbind via gravitational waves |
| ~34+ | >10³⁴ | Proton decay (if grand unification holds) dissolves complex matter |
| 38–68 | 10³⁸–10⁶⁸ | Black hole era; stellar-mass holes evaporate by floor 68 |
| 102 | 10¹⁰² | Largest supermassive holes finish evaporating — **top of the building** |
| 102–359 | — | Estimated window for Higgs vacuum decay |
| 10^68 | 10^(10⁶⁸) | Boltzmann brains become likely (unwritable exponent) |

**Rule**: predict this timeline in **broad strokes only**. Structure, not specifics.

## Entropy vs. Local Order — Decision Rules

| If you see… | Then… | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Order appearing spontaneously | Find the energy flow and the waste sink | Entropic two-step: local decrease requires larger export |
| No identifiable sink | Your accounting is wrong, not the second law | Total entropy never decreases |
| Entropy apparently vanishing | Look for the hidden ledger entry | Bekenstein: it went into horizon area |
| A low-entropy configuration | Suspect an organizing influence, not luck | Low entropy = rare = special |
| An entropy-raising change that isn't happening | Look for a missing catalyst | Entropic hurdles block accessible states |
| A system that must keep running | Ask how it purges waste heat | You are a steam engine; erasure, not thought, costs entropy |
| Gravity involved | Expect self-sustaining order | Negative heat capacity: losing heat makes it hotter |
| A far-future structure proposed | Check the sink still exists at that era | Expanding, cooling space eventually offers none |

**Threshold**: entropy = number of configurations that "pretty much look the same." Long/short, likely/unlikely have **no absolute meaning** — always state the comparison scale.

## Which Level of Explanation Applies Here?

| The question is about… | Answer at this level | Do NOT |
|---|---|---|
| What will happen next, physically | Particles and fields | Invoke purpose or will |
| Why this structure is possible at all | Entropy + energy flow | Claim it explains *which* structure |
| Why this variant persisted | Selection (incl. molecular Darwinism) | Assume adaptation before ruling out byproduct |
| What a person is doing and why | Instinct, memory, perception, social expectation | Say "it's just particles" — the insight isn't there |
| What a work of art means | Story, myth, artistic truth | Demand propositional verification |
| Whether something *matters* | Created meaning, inside minds | Look for it in the laws — it isn't there |

**Test**: layers must be *consistent* with those below, never *replaceable* by them. If your explanation dismisses a level, you've lost information; if it exempts a level from physics, you've lost the constraint.

## Fast Tells

- "It's all just particles" → eliminativism smuggled in as reductionism.
- "This trait evolved because it does X" → run the byproduct check first.
- "It feels wonderful" → proximate explanation only; the ultimate one is still owed.
- "That's too unlikely to matter" → over an open horizon, compute waiting time instead.
- "The theory implies I can't trust my evidence" → the model is incomplete (Boltzmann brains).
- "Coincidence, therefore significance" → pattern-detection outrunning analysis (the tarantula).
- "My legacy will endure" → symbolic immortality is real but bounded by the era of minds.
- "Do you believe X?" → reframe as calibrated confidence; then name literal vs. metaphorical register.
- Existential dread about the far future dismissed as silly → no principled basis for the dismissal (Scheffler, Wolf); the timescale intuition is parochial, not the worry.

## Meaning — the closing rules

1. The universe does not exist as an arena for life and mind; it will not hug you.
2. Value and meaning are **created inside subjective worlds**, not found in the laws.
3. Invented ≠ arbitrary: evolved predispositions + group feedback loops constrain what emerges.
4. **Finitude generates the calculus of value.** Unlimited do-overs would drain choices of consequence.
5. Species continuity, not personal continuity, is what secures the sense that things matter.
6. Equations have no intrinsic worth — "their worth derives from the minds they inhabit." Same for every artifact.
7. Look inward. "That is the noble direction to look."
