# Glossary — Keep Sharp

**ABCs of food** — GCBH framework: A-list (eat regularly), B-list (include), C-list (limit) foods (Ch7).
**ABCs of prevention (Isaacson)** — Anthropometrics, Biomarkers, Cognitive performance; reassessed every 6 months (Ch7, Ch10).
**Advance directive** — Legal document stating treatment/end-of-life wishes; includes living will and durable power of attorney for health care (Ch11).
**Alzheimer's disease** — Most common dementia (>half of cases); hallmarks are amyloid plaques and tau tangles (Ch2, Ch10).
**Amyloid (beta-amyloid)** — Sticky protein plaques that accumulate *between* neurons; healthy in normal form, harmful when misfolded (Ch2).
**Amyloid Cascade Hypothesis (ACH)** — Theory that plaque accumulation causes Alzheimer's; repeatedly failed in drug trials (Ch2).
**Analytical meditation** — Placing a problem in a mental "bubble" to isolate it from emotion and find clarity; taught by the Dalai Lama (Ch6).
**Anticholinergics** — Drugs blocking acetylcholine (Benadryl, some antidepressants); 3+ years' use linked to 54% higher dementia risk (Ch6, Ch10).
**APOE gene** — Risk gene with alleles E2 (protective), E3 (neutral), E4 (raises risk); not deterministic (Ch2, Ch10).
**BDNF (brain-derived neurotrophic factor)** — "Miracle-Gro for the brain"; boosted by exercise, sleep, stress reduction, sunlight, fasting (Ch3, Ch7).
**Blood-brain barrier** — Semipermeable wall protecting the brain; when leaky, toxins enter and worsen decline (Ch2).
**Blue Zone** — Region with unusually long-lived people (e.g., Okinawa) (Ch8).
**Chrono eating** — Eating aligned to daylight/circadian rhythm; frontloading calories (Ch7, Ch9).
**Circadian rhythm** — ~24-hr cycle governing sleep-wake, hormones, temperature (Ch6).
**Cognitive reserve (brain resiliency)** — The brain's ability to improvise around damage via built-up networks; from education, work, lifelong learning (Ch2, Ch5).
**Conservatorship / guardianship** — Court-appointed control when families can't agree; costly and abuse-prone (Ch11).
**Cortisol** — Stress hormone; chronic excess shrinks the hippocampus (Ch4, Ch6).
**CTE (chronic traumatic encephalopathy)** — Degenerative disease from repeated head blows; linked to tau (Ch2).
**Dementia** — Group of symptoms (memory loss, poor judgment), not one disease; spectrum from mild to severe; some causes reversible (Ch0, Ch10).
**Dendrite** — Branched extension of a neuron that receives signals; grows with novelty, entrenches with repetition (Ch1).
**Dirty Dozen (myths)** — The 12 destructive myths about the aging brain (Ch3).
**Encoding** — First memory phase: perception → hippocampus; requires attention (Ch1).
**Entourage effect** — Nutrients work best within whole food, not isolated supplements (Ch7).
**Flow** — State of total, energized immersion in an activity; requires purpose (Csikszentmihalyi) (Ch5).
**Ghrelin / leptin** — Appetite hormones (hunger / satiety); disrupted by poor sleep (Ch6).
**Glymphatic system** — The brain's self-cleaning drainage; runs in overdrive during sleep, clears amyloid (Ch6).
**Hippocampus** — Seahorse-shaped memory center; integrates sensory input; shrinks with stress/decline (Ch1).
**Hogeweyk** — Dutch "dementia village"; person-centered care model (Ch11).
**Ikigai** — Japanese "reason for being"; sense of purpose (Ch5).
**Inflammation** — Common thread across all brain-decline theories and most illness (Ch2).
**Intermittent fasting** — Limiting eating windows (12–16 hr); boosts BDNF and insulin sensitivity (Ch7, Ch9).
**Invisible second patient** — The caregiver, up to 6x more likely to develop dementia (Ch11).
**KAER process** — Gerontological Society four-step tool for detecting cognitive impairment (Ch10).
**MCI (mild cognitive impairment)** — Often a precursor to dementia; sometimes reversible (Ch4, Ch10).
**MIND diet** — Mediterranean–DASH Intervention for Neurodegenerative Delay; up to 53% lower Alzheimer's risk (Ch7).
**Moai** — Okinawan lifelong social support group (Ch8).
**Neurogenesis** — Birth of new neurons throughout life (proven 1998) (Ch1, Ch3).
**Neuroplasticity** — The brain's ability to rewire and reorganize connections; a two-way street (Ch1).
**Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus (NPH)** — Treatable dementia mimic; CSF buildup fixed with a shunt (Ch10).
**Omega-3 fatty acids** — Brain-nourishing fats (DHA, EPA, ALA); get from food, not supplements (Ch7).
**Pomodoro technique** — 25 min focused work + 5 min break; dopamine-reward productivity method (Ch6).
**Preclinical time** — 20–30 year window between brain changes and symptoms; prime intervention period (Ch10).
**Prions** — Proteins that trigger others (amyloid, tau) to misfold; universally fatal diseases (Ch2).
**Pseudodementia** — Dementia-like symptoms from severe depression; reversible when treated (Ch10).
**Retrieval** — Final memory phase: pulling info into conscious awareness (Ch1).
**S.H.A.R.P.** — Slash sugar, Hydrate smartly, Add omega-3s, Reduce portions, Plan ahead (Ch7).
**Sleep spindles** — Deep-sleep activity bursts moving memories from hippocampus to neocortex (Ch6).
**Sundowning** — Late-day confusion/agitation in ~20% of dementia patients (Ch11).
**SuperAgers** — 80+ people with memories as sharp as those decades younger (Ch3).
**Synapse** — Gap where neurons connect via neurotransmitters; strengthens with use (Ch1).
**Tau / neurofibrillary tangles** — Protein "railroad tracks" inside cells; knot up when damaged ("the bullet") (Ch2).
**Triple threat (Gary Small)** — Walk + friend + honest conversation (Ch8).
**Type 3 diabetes** — Nickname for Alzheimer's as an insulin-resistance disease of the brain (Ch2, Ch7).
**VO2 max** — Aerobic-fitness measure; higher fitness → healthier white matter → better cognition (Ch4).
**White matter** — Nerve-fiber bundles connecting gray-matter regions; health signals inter-region communication (Ch4).
