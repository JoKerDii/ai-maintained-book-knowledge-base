# Keep Sharp — Cheatsheet

## The one-line thesis
Cognitive decline is NOT inevitable. Lifestyle > genes (>90% of longevity is in your hands). Build the brain with 5 pillars; motivate by running *toward* a sharp brain, not away from fear.

## The 5 Pillars (memorize)
**M**ove · **D**iscover · **R**elax · **N**ourish · **C**onnect
> If you do only ONE thing: **Move.** Exercise is the *only* behavioral activity scientifically proven to help the brain.

## Decision rules
| When… | Do… | Because… |
|---|---|---|
| You can do only one brain-health thing | Exercise (break a sweat daily) | Only proven behavioral intervention; boosts BDNF |
| Motivating behavior change | Frame as running *toward* a goal | Fear activates amygdala → intense but transient |
| Choosing a "brain game" | Pick complex + novel + social learning you enjoy | Crosswords flex only word-finding; games are over-hyped |
| Memory "fails" on names | Fix attention (stop multitasking) or retrieval | Usually not systemic failure — both are trainable |
| Deciding whether to nap | Cap at 30 min, early afternoon | Longer/later naps wreck night sleep |
| Tempted by a supplement | Buy walking shoes or a pillow instead | No supplement reliably prevents dementia; industry unregulated |
| Someone offers "gluten-free will help your brain" | Ignore unless celiac/sensitivity | No evidence; low-gluten-low-whole-grain can raise heart risk |
| A relative gets a dementia diagnosis | Rule out mimics (NPH, meds, depression) first | Many "dementias" are treatable |
| Talking to someone with dementia | Never correct them; redirect | Forces impossible recall, causes distress |
| You're a caregiver | Put yourself on your to-do list | Caregivers are the "invisible second patient" (up to 6x risk) |

## Numbers & thresholds worth committing
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Exercise, minimum | 150 min/week; 30 min cardio × 5 days |
| Exercise, optimal longevity | ~450 min/week (~64 min/day, mostly walking) |
| Sitting break | ≥2 min every hour |
| Sleep | 7–8 hrs nightly (need is constant with age) |
| Caffeine cutoff | 2:00 p.m. |
| Last meal before bed | ~3 hours |
| Bedroom temp | 60–67°F |
| Warm light | 2700–3000K |
| Alcohol moderation | ≤2 drinks/day (men), ≤1 (women); prefer red wine |
| Colored foods/day | 7 different real-food colors |
| Purpose (ikigai) effect | ~20% lower future dementia risk |
| MIND diet adherence (top third) | 53% lower Alzheimer's risk (~7.5 yrs of aging) |
| Alzheimer's preclinical window | starts 20–30 yrs before symptoms |

## S.H.A.R.P. eating (fast recall)
**S**lash sugar · **H**ydrate smartly (drink instead of eat) · **A**dd omega-3s (from *food*) · **R**educe portions · **P**lan ahead

## Food ABCs
- **A (regularly)**: greens, berries, fish, healthy fats (EVOO/avocado/eggs), nuts/seeds
- **B (include)**: beans, whole fruits, low-fat dairy, poultry, whole grains
- **C (limit)**: fried, sweets, processed, red meat, high-fat dairy, salt

## Tells & smells (recognize the situation)
- **"Sitting is the new smoking"** → prolonged sitting (>8 hr) harms metabolically even if you're lean.
- **Sleep as a badge of honor** → red flag; no data links success to less sleep; you can't "game" the body clock.
- **Excessive daytime napping** → possible early Alzheimer's sign (tau in wakefulness centers).
- **Long-term anticholinergics** (Benadryl, "PM" meds) → 54% higher dementia risk at 3+ years.
- **Amyloid-riddled brain, no symptoms** → cognitive reserve at work; goal = "asymptomatic Alzheimer's."
- **Loneliness** → treat as a vital sign; social pain = physical pain in the brain.

## Daily productivity/rest toolkit (from Ch6)
- **Pomodoro**: 25 min focus / 5 min break.
- **Don't multitask** — single-task "like a surgeon"; guard mornings for creative work; no email first thing.
- **Marbles before sand**: schedule big commitments first.
- **Gratitude journal** + **15 min/day for yourself** + **daydream** (neural reset).
- **Analytical meditation** / **deep breathing** (exhale to count of 20) to lower cortisol.

## 12-week program in one line
Add one habit per pillar per week; never restart — accumulate. Get a baseline checkup, re-check at week 12. Set nonnegotiables. "Progress is better than perfection."
