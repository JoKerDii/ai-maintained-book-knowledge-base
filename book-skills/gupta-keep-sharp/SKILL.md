## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### The central thesis
**Cognitive decline is not inevitable.** The brain stays *plastic* and grows new cells (*neurogenesis*) throughout life. Over 90% of health and longevity is lifestyle, not genes. Alzheimer's begins **20–30 years before symptoms**, so prevention must start young. Motivate by running **toward** a sharp brain — never by fear (fear activates the amygdala → intense but transient action).

### The 5 Pillars of Brain Health (the book's backbone)
**M**ove · **D**iscover · **R**elax · **N**ourish · **C**onnect
1. **Move** (Ch4) — Exercise is the #1 lever and the *only* behavioral activity scientifically proven to help the brain. Min 150 min/week; optimal ~450 min/week (much can be brisk walking). "Break a sweat every day."
2. **Discover** (Ch5) — Build **cognitive reserve** through complex, novel, effortful, social learning. Cultivate purpose (**ikigai**) and **flow**. Crosswords alone don't cut it.
3. **Relax** (Ch6) — Restorative sleep (7–8 hrs; clears amyloid via the **glymphatic system**) + daily stress reduction (meditation, deep breathing, gratitude). Don't multitask.
4. **Nourish** (Ch7) — Eat MIND/Mediterranean style via **S.H.A.R.P.**; control blood sugar and inflammation; food over supplements (**entourage effect**).
5. **Connect** (Ch8) — High-quality relationships are "a vital sign." Quality > quantity; loneliness is toxic (social pain = physical pain).

### S.H.A.R.P. — the eating framework (Ch7)
**S**lash sugar · **H**ydrate smartly (drink instead of eat) · **A**dd omega-3s (from food) · **R**educe portions · **P**lan ahead.

### Master mental models
- **Explain the "why," not just the what** — understanding mechanisms makes habits stick.
- **Prevention → Early Intervention → Optimization** — the three camps for addressing decline; medicine has been too passive.
- **Treat the patient, not the brain scan** — an aged-looking brain can function beautifully; the goal is "asymptomatic Alzheimer's."
- **Inflammation and blood sugar** are the two master levers running through every decline pathway.
- **Attention is the gate to memory** — divided attention (multitasking) sabotages encoding.
- Build a better brain **like any other muscle** — meaningful change happens in ~12 weeks.

### The 12-week program (Ch9)
Add one habit per pillar per week; never restart — *accumulate*. Baseline checkup → re-check at week 12. Set nonnegotiables. "Progress is better than perfection."
