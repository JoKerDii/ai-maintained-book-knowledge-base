# Cheatsheet — The 48 Laws of Power

Decision rules distilled from Greene's judgment. Use to pick a move fast. (Ethical note: this is descriptive strategy, much of it Machiavellian — read as a lens, not a mandate.)

## Master Decision Rules (the meta-logic)
- **Force vs. seduction:** if you *can* make them *want* it, never coerce it (43 > force). Coercion breeds backlash; seduction breeds loyal pawns.
- **Half-measures rule:** with a real enemy, either finish completely (15) or don't strike. A survivor is a future war.
- **Attention dial:** too invisible → court attention (6); too common → manufacture absence (16). Manage visibility like a price.
- **Ask framing:** every request routes through the other's self-interest (13), never your need or their pity.
- **Peak danger:** the moment of victory is the moment of greatest peril — stop (47), stay clean (26), don't get drunk on success.
- **Independence rule:** owe no one (40), commit to no side (20), keep no fixed form (48) — freedom is the base of power.

## Tells & Smells (recognize the situation)
- You feel an urge to explain/justify → you're about to violate Law 4. Say less.
- You're reminding someone they owe you → dead lever; switch to their self-interest (13).
- You just won an argument and they went quiet-angry → Pyrrhic; you lost (9).
- You feel safest cutting everyone off → isolation trap (18); the danger is the opposite.
- A "friend" you elevated seems resentful → Law 2 in motion; envy precedes betrayal.
- Someone keeps giving you free things/favors → you're being indebted (40) or Trojan-horsed (12).
- A rival is baiting your temper → they're stirring waters (39); go calm.
- You're dazzled by someone's spectacle → look at what they're actually doing (37).
